## Win the objective in Gem Grab

Brawl Stars · 3v3 Gem Grab · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
3v3 · patch not playtested

In standard 3v3 Gem Grab, collect 10 gems as a team and hold them until the countdown ends.

Before you start: Standard 3v3 Gem Grab.

1. Collect gems from the centre of the map or from defeated opponents.
2. Once your team has enough gems, protect the players carrying them.
3. Keep the winning gem total until the countdown finishes. Watch the objective, not just eliminations.

Success check: Your team holds the required gems through the countdown.

Troubleshooting: Confirm the mode; variants use different targets.

Special 5v5 and multi-team versions can use different gem targets. This guide covers standard 3v3.

## Plan when to use your Gadget again

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · current Gadget cooldown system · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher help checked 15 Oct 2026

Gadgets are active abilities with cooldowns; do not follow old fixed-charge advice.

Before you start: A Brawler with an unlocked, owned Gadget equipped.

1. Open your Brawler screen and select an owned Gadget.
2. Read its effect and any activation conditions.
3. Enter a suitable practice or match mode.
4. Use the Gadget when its effect fits the situation.
5. Watch the timer on the Gadget icon or below the Brawler.
6. Use it again after the cooldown when another useful opportunity appears.

Success check: You recognize when the Gadget is ready for another use.

Troubleshooting: No Gadget available? Check the Brawler unlock requirement and ownership. Button unavailable? Wait for the cooldown and check the ability conditions.

Cooldowns vary by Gadget. This guide does not recommend purchasing anything or rank particular builds.

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher support checked 20 October 2026

Charge it through attacks, then activate the purple button when you can use its benefit.

Before you start: A Brawler with an unlocked, usable Hypercharge.

1. Open the Brawler page and read its Hypercharge effect.

## Join the same team with a Team Code

Brawl Stars · Standard client · team lobby · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Team lobby · publisher support checked 25 October 2026

Tap + beside your Brawler. The host chooses Create; joining players choose Join and use the shared Team Code.

Before you start: Brawl Stars accounts able to access team play and a current team code.

1. The host taps + beside their Brawler, then Create.
2. Share the displayed Team Code, QR code or link with the intended teammates.
3. Each joining player taps +, then Join, and uses the shared option.
4. The host chooses the event; everyone confirms I’m Ready to start matchmaking.

Success check: The intended players appear in the same team lobby before matchmaking begins.

Troubleshooting: Only the host selects the event. Check the code and available team slots if joining fails.

A normal team lobby is separate from Friendly Battle practice. Invitations and event availability can have restrictions.
