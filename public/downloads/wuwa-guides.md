## Turn a dodge into a counterattack

Wuthering Waves · Standard combat · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-06. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Patch not playtested

After a successful dodge, immediately use a Basic Attack to perform a Dodge Counter.

Before you start: A familiar enemy and access to Basic Attack.

1. Watch an enemy’s attack and time your dodge as the hit approaches.
2. After successfully avoiding it, immediately press Basic Attack.
3. Practice the timing with a familiar enemy before trying a harder fight.

Success check: A Dodge Counter animation follows the dodge.

Troubleshooting: Check the timing and your character’s attack description.

This is a community-sourced basic mechanic. Character-specific attacks may behave differently.

## Recognize a parry opportunity

Wuthering Waves · Standard combat · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-06. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
General combat · current patch not tested

Watch for an enemy attack that signals a parry opportunity, then time an eligible attack to meet it.

Before you start: An eligible attack and an enemy move that can be parried.

1. Watch the enemy’s attack animation for a parry cue.
2. Use a suitable attack as the incoming strike reaches you; practice against a familiar enemy.
3. If the timing or attack type does not work, dodge and check that character’s move description.

Success check: The incoming move is interrupted by the parry.

Troubleshooting: If you do not see a parry cue, practise dodging that move instead.

Not every enemy move or character attack can parry. This is a general mechanic, not a guaranteed answer for a particular boss.

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.

Activate a Resonance Beacon in person, then select the activated beacon on your map to travel back.

## Track a Resonator's ascension materials

Wuthering Waves · Open-world material tracking · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Collection Spots · introduced in1.3

Select an ascension material in Backpack or the Resonators screen, then open Collection Spots.

Before you start: A supported ascension material and access to its region.

1. Find the material needed for your planned ascension.
2. Select its icon in Backpack or the Resonators screen.
3. Choose Collection Spots to show a location on the map.
4. Collect the material there; after that spot is exhausted, the next location becomes trackable.

Success check: The map shows a material collection location.

Troubleshooting: Check region access and the item's available source options if Collection Spots is unavailable.

Official Version1.3 notes document this feature. Tracking locations is not a promise that all materials are available immediately or that every region is unlocked.

## Save and compare an Echo preset

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · Echo Assembly · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Echo Preset documented in Version 2.1

Save Current Setup in Echo Preset, then compare before applying another.

Before you start: Owned Echoes and access to Echo Assembly.

1. Open the Resonator’s Echo Assembly interface.
2. Select the Echo Preset icon at the bottom right.
3. Choose Current Setup and Save As Preset.
4. Give the setup a name that explains its purpose.
5. Select a saved preset and expand the attribute comparison.
6. Use Apply only when you want that lineup on the current Resonator.

Success check: The named preset appears and its applied lineup matches your intention.

Troubleshooting: Wrong stats? Compare the saved preset against Current Setup before applying. Expected a weapon change? This feature saves an Echo lineup; inspect weapon equipment separately.

A saved preset preserves a choice; it does not establish that the build is optimal.

## Protect useful Echo drops with an auto-lock plan

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · Data Bank / Echo Management · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Echo Management documented in Version 2.5

Create an Auto-Lock plan in Data Bank → Data Merge → Echo Management.

Before you start: Echo Management unlocked and a clear idea of the stats you want to keep.

1. Open Data Bank, then Data Merge and Echo Management.
2. Create a plan for Echoes you want to keep.
3. Set quality, COST, Sonata Effect and Mainstat criteria as needed.
4. Enable the intended plan and check any other active plans.
5. Obtain a new Echo and inspect whether its lock matches the rule.
6. Adjust overly broad criteria before adding an Auto-Discard plan.

Success check: A matching new Echo receives the intended lock.

Troubleshooting: Unexpected lock? More than one plan may be active. Lock and discard both match? The documented priority is to lock the Echo.

Auto-Discard marks an Echo for discard. Review marked items before performing a separate consuming action.

## Make upgrade materials from the Terminal

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · Synthesis on the Go · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Terminal Synthesis documented in Version 2.0

Use Terminal → Synthesis and choose Purification or Conversion deliberately.

Before you start: Access to Synthesis and the ingredients shown by an available recipe.

1. Read the exact material and rarity needed on your upgrade screen.
2. Open the Terminal and select Synthesis.
3. Use Purification when the recipe combines materials across rarities.
4. Use Conversion only for a listed exchange within the same category and rarity.
5. Inspect the ingredients, quantity and resulting item before confirming.
6. Return to the upgrade screen and check your available amount.

Success check: Your inventory contains the intended upgrade material.

Troubleshooting: Recipe unavailable? Only listed recipes are supported; an arbitrary item cannot be converted. Still short? Recheck both rarity and required quantity before using more materials.

The menu and recipe shown in the current game take priority over old material lists.

## Check stored Waveplate Crystals before farming

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · standard overworld and menus · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Waveplate Crystal storage documented in Version 1.2

Read both energy counts on the Map, then exchange stored Crystals if needed.

Before you start: Waveplate Crystals already stored and an available reward activity.

1. Open the Map and inspect the Waveplate and Waveplate Crystal counts.
2. Choose a challenge whose materials you actually need.
3. Compare its reward cost with your available Waveplates.
4. If short, use the energy replenishment prompts to exchange stored Crystals.
5. Check the new total before entering or claiming the reward.

Success check: You know whether your stored energy covers the planned claim.

Troubleshooting: No Crystals stored? They accumulate after normal Waveplates reach their cap. Not enough energy? Exchange only the stored amount you have; this is not unlimited energy.

## Learn a Resonator in Skill Training

Wuthering Waves · Resonator Skill Training · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Skill Training menu · documented in Version 2.5

Open Guidebook → Path of Growth → Skill Training and select an available Resonator tutorial.

Before you start: Access to Skill Training and an available Resonator tutorial.

1. Open Guidebook, then Path of Growth and Skill Training.
2. Choose the Resonator you want to learn.
3. Follow the tutorial’s skill prompts and complete its checks.
4. Repeat the sequence until you understand the required inputs, then try it in a familiar fight.

Success check: You complete the tutorial and can describe the skill sequence it taught.

Troubleshooting: Newly acquired Resonators may appear at the top and later return to their usual list position. Search the list before assuming a tutorial disappeared.

The menu and sorting behavior are documented in Version 2.5. A tutorial is a learning aid, not proof of a best team or damage rotation.
