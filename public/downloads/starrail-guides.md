## Break an enemy’s Toughness

Honkai: Star Rail · Standard combat · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Patch not playtested

Attack with a damage type matching an enemy’s weakness to lower its Toughness bar.

Before you start: A matching damage type available in your party.

1. Check the weakness symbols displayed above the enemy.
2. Use a character whose attack matches one of those types.
3. Keep reducing the white Toughness bar until Weakness Break triggers.

Success check: The enemy’s white Toughness bar decreases.

Troubleshooting: Inspect battle effects if the enemy’s Toughness is protected.

Some enemies and abilities change how breaking works. Check the battle’s effect descriptions.

## Recover Skill Points in battle

Honkai: Star Rail · Standard combat · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
General combat · current patch not tested

A normal Basic Attack generally restores one team Skill Point; most Skills spend one.

Before you start: A character with a normal Basic Attack available.

1. Look at the team Skill Point counter during combat.
2. When it is low, use a character’s Basic Attack instead of a Skill.
3. Use the restored point for the character whose Skill you need next.

Success check: The team’s Skill Point counter increases.

Troubleshooting: Enhanced Basic Attacks may follow different rules. Read that ability.

Some abilities change Skill Point costs or gains; read the current character description.

Honkai: Star Rail · Assignments · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Claim All workflow documented in Version 2.1

Review your stock, claim finished returns and redispatch deliberately.

Before you start: Assignments available on your account.

1. Open Assignments.
2. Inspect the material stock shown for the assignments you use.

## Review a Relic set with Smart Equip

Honkai: Star Rail · Character equipment · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Smart Equip workflow documented in Version 3.5

Smart Equip is a starting point; compare every replacement with your intended build.

Before you start: Owned Relics and access to Character > Relics.

1. Open the character you want to equip.
2. Choose Relics, then Smart Equip.
3. Review the suggested pieces and their main stats.
4. Select individual slots to replace pieces you do not want.
5. Check selected pieces, which are marked and prioritized in recommendations.
6. Apply the choice and inspect the resulting equipment. The panel stays open for further adjustments.

Success check: The character is wearing the pieces you reviewed.

Troubleshooting: An unwanted piece keeps appearing: Review selected or prioritized pieces and replace the affected slot. Recommendation does not suit your team: Compare the character’s abilities and your own build goal; automatic suggestions are not a universal best build.

Version 3.5 also merged Wishful Resin into Self-Modeling Resin. Older crafting instructions may therefore show an obsolete resource.

Sources: [Honkai: Star Rail · Version 3.5 equipment changes](https://hsr.hoyoverse.com/en-us/news/158598)

## Request eligible salvaged equipment back

Honkai: Star Rail · 5-star Relics / 4-star Light Cones · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Support article updated 12 Nov 2025

Open Smartphone > Bug Report > Relic/Light Cone Salvage Retrieval and check eligibility.

Before you start: An accidentally salvaged 5-star Relic or 4-star Light Cone.

1. Confirm whether the lost item was salvaged and check its rarity.
2. Open the Smartphone menu.
3. Choose Bug Report.
4. Open Relic/Light Cone Salvage Retrieval.
5. Read its current retrieval rules and conditions.
6. If eligible, complete the in-game request and check its result before assuming recovery.

Success check: You have an eligibility result or a submitted retrieval request.

Troubleshooting: Item is not listed: Check the form’s current conditions; recovery is not guaranteed. Different rarity or loss method: This help article only establishes the named salvage categories. Use normal Support for other cases.

The guide does not promise unlimited recovery or invent a retrieval deadline.

## Heal by attacking Gallagher’s marked enemy

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Gallagher · base mechanic documented in Version 2.1

Gallagher’s Ultimate applies Besotted. An ally who attacks a Besotted enemy restores their own HP.

Before you start: Gallagher in the team, an available Ultimate and an ally missing HP.

1. Read Gallagher’s current Ultimate and Talent descriptions.
2. Use his Ultimate and inspect an enemy for Besotted.
3. On the injured ally’s action, attack an enemy still affected by Besotted.
4. Watch that attacker’s HP after the attack.

Success check: The attacking ally recovers HP when the documented condition is met.

Troubleshooting: Check that the enemy is still marked and that the injured character actually attacked it.

This covers the base mechanic described at Gallagher’s release. It does not specify healing amounts, Eidolon effects or an optimal rotation.
