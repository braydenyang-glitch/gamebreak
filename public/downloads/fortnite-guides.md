## Can’t pick up items?

Fortnite · Battle Royale · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Battle Royale · patch not playtested

Check for conflicting Sprint and Use bindings. Epic lists restoring default controls as a workaround.

Before you start: A copy or screenshot of your current custom controls.

1. Open Settings and find the controls for your keyboard or controller.
2. Check whether Sprint and Use share the same input. Record your custom bindings first.
3. Try Restore to Default, then test item pickup again. Reapply custom controls carefully.

Success check: Use correctly picks up an item.

Troubleshooting: If resetting controls does not help, collect a bug report instead of repeatedly resetting.

The issue may be fixed in a later patch. This workaround has not been tested by GameBreak.

## Keep your loadout in a predictable order

Fortnite · Battle Royale / Zero Build · Preferred Item Slots · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Feature documented in v17.20

Assign categories in Settings → Game → Preferred Item Slots.

Before you start: Access to Game settings and a supported Battle Royale playlist.

1. Open Settings and select the Game tab.
2. Find Preferred Item Slots and choose Configure.
3. Assign your commonly used weapon categories to the slots you prefer.
4. Leave a slot for healing or other consumables, then apply the changes.
5. In a match, keep an inventory slot empty and pick up a matching item.
6. Check its position before practising the corresponding weapon-switch control.

Success check: A matching pickup goes to the intended slot.

Troubleshooting: Inventory full? The documented routing requires at least one empty slot. Consumables moving right? Review Auto-Sort Consumables to Right as well.

This is category routing, not a saved list of particular guns. Check your installed client if menus differ.

## Review one mistake in a match replay

Fortnite · Battle Royale · PC / PlayStation / Xbox replay support · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Replay support article

Open Career → Replays and choose a recent recording.

Before you start: A supported platform and a match recorded by the current game version.

1. In Settings → Game, check that the appropriate replay recording option is enabled.
2. Play a match, then return to the lobby.
3. Open Career and choose Replays.
4. Select the recent match and press Play.
5. Review the moment before elimination; look for cover and the safe-zone route.
6. Write one change to try in your next match. Rename a replay if you want to keep it.

Success check: The replay plays and you identify one practical adjustment.

Troubleshooting: No recording? Enable recording before the next match; it cannot recreate an unrecorded match. Old file will not open? Replays depend on the game version; saving does not guarantee compatibility.

Replay review is a suggested practice exercise. It does not automatically prove why a match was lost.

## Cross a short obstacle with sprint and mantle

Fortnite · Battle Royale / Zero Build · core movement · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Sprint and mantle documented March 2022

Sprint toward a reachable ledge and jump close enough to grab it.

Before you start: A quiet area, a low ledge and some sprint stamina.

1. Check your Sprint and Jump bindings.
2. Choose a low obstacle with clear ground beyond it.
3. Start sprinting and watch the stamina meter.
4. Jump toward the ledge so your character can grab it.
5. Let the mantle finish, then check your position before moving again.
6. Repeat from the same spot to learn a reliable approach.

Success check: You get onto the ledge without stopping beneath it.

Troubleshooting: Sprint stops early? Let stamina recover before repeating. No grab? Try a lower ledge or jump closer; mantle has a reach limit.

Source checked; not gameplay tested. Use the current in-game prompts if controls differ.

Sliding documented December 2021

Hold Crouch while running to start a slide.

## Practise a wall scramble and wall kick

Fortnite · Battle Royale / Zero Build · Chapter 6 movement · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Wall Scramble and Wall Kick documented December 2024

Jump at a wall in front to scramble; jump against a wall beside you to kick away.

Before you start: A quiet location with a low vertical wall and safe landing space.

1. Approach the wall directly with room to land safely.
2. Jump toward it to start a Wall Scramble.
3. If a reachable ledge is above, let the automatic mantle complete.
4. Repeat, pressing Jump during the scramble to kick away.
5. Try a separate approach with the wall beside you while airborne.
6. Jump against that side wall and steer toward the clear landing area.

Success check: You can distinguish climbing toward a ledge from launching away.

Troubleshooting: No mantle? The wall may not have a reachable ledge. Unexpected direction? Reset your approach; front and side contact produce different moves.

Source checked; not gameplay tested. Use the current in-game prompts if controls differ.

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.

Enable Gyro Aiming in Touch and Motion settings.

Before you start: Compatible PlayStation, Switch, PC controller or Android motion controls.

1. Open Settings → Touch and Motion.
2. Turn Gyro Aiming on.

## Use a Reboot Card to return a teammate

Fortnite · Battle Royale · supported team playlists · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Battle Royale reboot fundamentals · introduced in v8.30; current timers untested

When your playlist provides Reboot Cards, collect your eliminated teammate’s card and use an available Reboot Van.

Before you start: A team playlist with the reboot mechanic and a collectible teammate card.

1. Check the eliminated teammate’s card marker and current expiry timer.
2. Collect the card before it expires if you can reach it safely.
3. Locate a usable Reboot Van and check its current availability.
4. Follow the van’s interaction prompt until the reboot completes.

Success check: The eliminated teammate returns to play through the reboot.

Troubleshooting: An unavailable van may be cooling down; some playlists do not use this mechanic.

The source establishes the original mechanic. Do not use its old timer, starting gear or map locations as current values. Reload has different reboot rules.
