## Learn a Resonator in Skill Training

Wuthering Waves · Resonator Skill Training · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Skill Training menu · documented in Version 2.5

Open Guidebook → Path of Growth → Skill Training and select an available Resonator tutorial.

Before you start: Access to Skill Training and an available Resonator tutorial.

1. Open Guidebook, then Path of Growth and Skill Training.
2. Choose the Resonator you want to learn.
3. Follow the tutorial’s skill prompts and complete its checks.
4. Repeat the sequence until you understand the required inputs, then try it in a familiar fight.

Success check: You complete the tutorial and can describe the skill sequence it taught.

Troubleshooting: Newly acquired Resonators may appear at the top and later return to their usual list position. Search the list before assuming a tutorial disappeared.

The menu and sorting behavior are documented in Version 2.5. A tutorial is a learning aid, not proof of a best team or damage rotation.
