## Compare HRTF for directional audio

Delta Force · PC · Audio settings · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC · Audio settings

HRTF is the game’s directional binaural audio option.

Before you start: Headphones and access to Audio settings.

1. Record your current Audio settings.
2. Find HRTF in the Audio menu.
3. Enable it and listen to sounds from different directions.
4. If possible, compare a teammate moving above and below you.
5. Repeat with HRTF disabled at the same volume.
6. Keep the setting that makes direction clearer on your setup.

Success check: You have compared the two settings using similar sounds.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

The comparison procedure is editorial. No listening test was performed for this guide.
