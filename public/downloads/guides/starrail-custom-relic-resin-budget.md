## Budget Resin for a customized relic

Honkai: Star Rail · Customized Relic Synthesis · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Customized Relic Synthesis

Main stat plus one substat costs two Resin; plus two costs five.

Before you start: Customized Synthesis access and Self-Modeling Resin.

1. Decide which set and slot you actually need.
2. Select the required main stat in Customized Synthesis.
3. Choose one or two substats according to your budget.
4. Compare the displayed total with your available Self-Modeling Resin.
5. Confirm the selected stats before creating the relic.
6. Inspect the new relic before enhancing it.

Success check: The created relic has the selected stats and the expected cost was paid.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Selecting initial stats does not promise particular later enhancement rolls.
