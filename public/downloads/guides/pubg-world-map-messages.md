## Send a map message without voice chat

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · map communication · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC · controls documented in42.2

On the PC World Map, hold the right mouse button or mouse wheel to open the Message Radial.

Before you start: A team match and access to the World Map.

1. Open the World Map; the documented default PC shortcut is M.
2. Hold right mouse button or the mouse wheel at the relevant map location.
3. Choose the appropriate message or marker and confirm it.
4. Check the displayed marker before your team moves.

Success check: The intended marker/message appears for your team.

Troubleshooting: Check custom bindings if the documented input opens another action.

Controls documented in Update42.2, published2026-07-14. Check your own bindings; this is PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS PC, not PUBG Mobile.
