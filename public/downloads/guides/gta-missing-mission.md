## No new story missions appearing?

Grand Theft Auto V · Story mode · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Story mode · patch not playtested

Try all three characters and allow some in-game time to pass. Some missions depend on a character or phone call.

Before you start: Story Mode and an earlier save to keep.

1. Switch between the three protagonists and check their map markers.
2. Do another activity and move to another area while waiting for a mission call.
3. If still stuck, use Quick Save in a new slot, then load that new save. Keep your older saves.

Success check: The required mission or call becomes available.

Troubleshooting: Check each protagonist again. These steps cannot repair every save problem.

Rockstar lists these as possible workarounds, not a guaranteed fix. This guide is for Story Mode only.
