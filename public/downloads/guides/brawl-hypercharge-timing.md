## Use a charged Hypercharge deliberately

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · standard client · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher support checked 20 October 2026

Charge it through attacks, then activate the purple button when you can use its benefit.

Before you start: A Brawler with an unlocked, usable Hypercharge.

1. Open the Brawler page and read its Hypercharge effect.
2. Check that the Brawler meets the required Power Level.
3. In battle, land attacks to build the Hypercharge meter.
4. Move into a useful position before spending the charge.
5. Activate the purple button and use the enhanced abilities while it lasts.
6. After it ends, resume charging rather than repeatedly tapping an empty button.

Success check: You use the enhanced window for an intended action.

Troubleshooting: Owned but unusable? Hypercharge use requires Power Level 11. Meter fills slowly? It charges more slowly than a normal Super.
