## Start Temporary Acclimatization

Genshin Impact · Chenyu Vale / Fontaine · World Quest · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Quest-specific prerequisites

Complete the required Chenyu and Fontaine quests, then speak with Woliu at Yilong Wharf.

Before you start: Access to Chenyu Vale and Fontaine.

1. Check progress in Chenyu’s Blessings of Sunken Jade.
2. Finish its required part, Qiaoying of the Sacred Mountain.
3. Near the Opera Epiclese in Fontaine, check the quest In Expert Company? (II).
4. Complete In Expert Company? (II); merely accepting it is insufficient.
5. Travel to Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale.
6. Find Woliu near the pavilion and speak with her to start Temporary Acclimatization.

Success check: Temporary Acclimatization begins and its objective appears.

Troubleshooting: Woliu offers no quest: Recheck both prerequisite completions, including part II in Fontaine. At the wrong harbour: The start is Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale, not the Opera Epiclese.

This guide covers unlocking the quest only; it is not a walkthrough of every later objective.
