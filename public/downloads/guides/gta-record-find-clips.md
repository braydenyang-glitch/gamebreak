## Record a clip and find it in Rockstar Editor

Grand Theft Auto V · PC · Story Mode Rockstar Editor · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC · Story Mode Rockstar Editor

Use the recording prompts, then open Clip Management.

Before you start: PC version with Rockstar Editor available.

1. Hold Alt to show recording options with default keyboard controls.
2. Press F1 to start a manual recording.
3. Record the short event you want to review.
4. Press F2 to stop recording.
5. In Story Mode, open Pause and the Rockstar Editor tab.
6. Open Clip Management and locate the saved clip.

Success check: The clip is listed in Clip Management.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Recording and publishing are separate; this guide only creates a local clip.
