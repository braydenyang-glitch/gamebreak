## Reduce intense first-person camera movement

Grand Theft Auto V · Story Mode · first-person-capable editions · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
First-person settings; platform menus can differ

Reduce head movement and choose whether cover should use third person.

Before you start: An edition with First Person Mode.

1. Open Settings > Controls and locate the first-person options.
2. Turn First Person Head Bobbing off if running movement feels distracting.
3. Turn First Person Ragdoll off to reduce forced first-person motion when hit.
4. Turn First Person Combat Rolls off if rolling movement is uncomfortable.
5. Set First Person Third Person Cover on if you prefer a wider view while using cover.
6. Return to play and test walking, jogging and cover; adjust one setting at a time.

Success check: Camera movement and cover perspective match your preference.

Troubleshooting: Cover still stays first person: Recheck the specific cover option, rather than only the overall camera mode. Option missing: Confirm your edition supports first person; the original PS3/Xbox 360 versions do not use this feature set.

These are comfort preferences, not a guaranteed performance or aiming improvement. Use your edition’s displayed menu labels.
