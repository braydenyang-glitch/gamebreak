## Turn Department missions into an upgrade plan

Delta Force · Operations · Garena documentation · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Department, Supply Station and Black Site progression

Check an unlock requirement before choosing your next Department mission.

Before you start: Access to Operations progression menus.

1. Inspect the Supply Station item or Black Site upgrade you want.
2. Note any Department-level requirement.
3. Open that Department and read available mission objectives.
4. Choose a mission you can complete with your available equipment.
5. Finish it, collect its reward and recheck the unlock requirement.

Success check: You know which mission advances your selected unlock.

Troubleshooting: Item is still locked: Compare the required Department level with your current level. Upgrade still unavailable: Review its remaining requirements; Department progress is not the only possible condition.

Based on Garena documentation; check regional-client requirements. No universal mission order is claimed.
