## Join a friend in the same experience

Roblox · DOORS · Roblox platform · DOORS preparation · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Roblox platform · joining permissions

Open your friend's Roblox profile and choose Join Experience when their permissions allow it.

Before you start: A friend in an experience that you are permitted to join.

1. Confirm which experience your friend is playing.
2. Find their username and open their profile.
3. Use Join Experience if it is offered.
4. After loading, confirm you reached the intended experience before beginning.

Success check: You enter the friend's experience.

Troubleshooting: A missing Join option can be a permission setting, not a GameBreak or DOORS bug.

Joining requires the appropriate permissions and the friend's join setting. This does not promise entry into an already-started DOORS floor or private run.
