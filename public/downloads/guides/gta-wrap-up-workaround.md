## The Wrap Up freezes after its first cutscene?

Grand Theft Auto V · Story mode · The Wrap Up · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Story Mode · historical documented workaround

Rockstar's documented workaround is to enter the first cutscene on foot instead of inside a vehicle.

Before you start: The specific vehicle/cutscene problem in The Wrap Up.

1. Keep a separate Story Mode save before troubleshooting.
2. Return to the mission start and leave your vehicle.
3. Enter the starting cutscene on foot, then check whether control returns normally.

Success check: The mission proceeds after the first cutscene.

Troubleshooting: If the symptom differs, preserve the save and use Rockstar Support rather than repeating unrelated fixes.

This is a historical workaround from a support article updated2024-03-12. It is not evidence that the bug still affects every current edition.
