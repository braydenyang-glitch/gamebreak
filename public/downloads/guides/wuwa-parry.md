## Recognize a parry opportunity

Wuthering Waves · Standard combat · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-06. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
General combat · current patch not tested

Watch for an enemy attack that signals a parry opportunity, then time an eligible attack to meet it.

Before you start: An eligible attack and an enemy move that can be parried.

1. Watch the enemy’s attack animation for a parry cue.
2. Use a suitable attack as the incoming strike reaches you; practice against a familiar enemy.
3. If the timing or attack type does not work, dodge and check that character’s move description.

Success check: The incoming move is interrupted by the parry.

Troubleshooting: If you do not see a parry cue, practise dodging that move instead.

Not every enemy move or character attack can parry. This is a general mechanic, not a guaranteed answer for a particular boss.
