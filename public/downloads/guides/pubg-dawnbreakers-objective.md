## Complete the Dawnbreakers defense objective

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · Arcade · SLB: Dawnbreakers of Rondo · limited-time PvE · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Official announcement published 14 October 2026

Fuel the Generator, defend the connected Capacitor, and survive until its signal is ready.

Before you start: The SLB playlist during its published service window.

1. Open Play → Arcade → SLB: Dawnbreakers of Rondo.
2. Choose a difficulty with your team and ready up.
3. Collect a basic weapon from the stage’s Vending Machine.
4. Follow the Generator cables to locate the defense objective.
5. Find Gas Cans and fuel the Generator.
6. Protect the Capacitor from Hungers while it charges.
7. Keep at least one teammate alive and complete the escape objective.

Success check: The Capacitor sends its signal and the stage completes.

Troubleshooting: Charge reset? Hungers destroyed the activated Capacitor. Playlist absent? Its PC schedule ends 4 November 2026 at 00:00 UTC.

PC service: 14 October after maintenance to 4 November 2026, 00:00 UTC. This is PvE, not standard Battle Royale.
