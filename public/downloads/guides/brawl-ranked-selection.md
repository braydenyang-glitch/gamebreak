## Prepare for Ranked selection

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · standard client · Ranked · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher support checked 20 October 2026

Open Ranked, inspect the map and mode, then choose an eligible Brawler.

Before you start: Ranked unlocked and enough eligible Brawlers for the current rank.

1. Select Ranked from the Events page.
2. Read the current rank’s Brawler and Power Level requirements.
3. Check which temporary maxed Brawlers the season offers.
4. After matchmaking, inspect the displayed mode and map.
5. Follow the ban or pick phase shown for your rank.
6. Choose a role you understand and complete the match.

Success check: You enter with an eligible pick and understand the objective.

Troubleshooting: A Brawler is unavailable? Check its level and the rank’s roster requirements. Selection order changed? Higher ranks use different ban, pick and round formats.
