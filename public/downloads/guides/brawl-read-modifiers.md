## Adjust your plan for an event modifier

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · standard client · events with modifiers · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher support checked 20 October 2026

Read the active modifier and change one part of your plan.

Before you start: An available event with its modifier information.

1. Select the event and inspect its active modifier.
2. Check whether it changes health, terrain, speed or visibility.
3. For Top Dog, plan around Hot Dogs because normal regeneration stops.
4. For Energy Drinks, remember that the boost does not increase health.
5. Choose a suitable Brawler and use the new rule during the match.
6. Recheck the rules when switching events.

Success check: Your strategy accounts for the selected event’s special rule.

Troubleshooting: Healing does not behave normally? The modifier may change it. Extra drinks gave no extra strength? Their effects refresh rather than stacking extra damage or speed.
