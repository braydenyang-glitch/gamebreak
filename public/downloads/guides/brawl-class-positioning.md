## Match your position to your Brawler’s role

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · standard client · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher support checked 20 October 2026

Read the class, inspect the map, and choose a lane that fits the Brawler’s attacks.

Before you start: An owned Brawler and its ability descriptions.

1. Open the Brawler view and read the class at the top left.
2. Check whether its attacks need distance, close contact or cover.
3. Choose a route that supports those attacks.
4. With Artillery, look for useful cover to throw over; with Marksmen, preserve distance.
5. With Support, stay where your abilities can reach allies.
6. After a defeat, reconsider your position before changing your whole loadout.

Success check: You can explain why your chosen position suits the Brawler.

Troubleshooting: Class advice fails in one matchup? Read the individual kit; classes are broad descriptions. Cannot help allies? Move with the team instead of chasing alone.
