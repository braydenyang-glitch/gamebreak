## Lower World Level when exploration is too difficult

Genshin Impact · World Level 3 or above · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
World Level 3 or above

A reduction lowers World Level by one; changes have a 24-hour cooldown.

Before you start: World Level 3+ and no active change cooldown.

1. Check your current World Level in the Paimon menu.
2. Select the information icon beside World Level.
3. Read the lower enemy levels and reduced reward warning.
4. Choose the option to lower World Level and confirm.
5. Retry a previously difficult open-world encounter with your prepared team.

Success check: The Paimon menu shows the reduced World Level.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This is an open-world adjustment, not a promise that every fixed-level challenge becomes easier.
