Repair-related maximum-durability loss depends on the amount repaired.

Before you start: Damaged gear and access to repair options.

1. Inspect the gear’s current and maximum durability.
2. Check which repair supplies are compatible.
3. Review the displayed repair amount, cost and durability outcome.
4. Compare that result with an affordable replacement.
5. Choose the better option for your planned raid.
6. Recheck durability after confirming the repair.

Success check: You know the usable durability gained for the cost.
