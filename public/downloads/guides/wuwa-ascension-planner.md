## Plan one Resonator’s next upgrades

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · standard overworld and menus · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Resonator upgrade planner documented in Version 2.7

Open Terminal → Resonators → the ascension planner and review its modules.

Before you start: Access to the Resonator planner and a character you want to improve.

1. Open Terminal → Resonators and enter the ascension planner.
2. Select the Resonator you want to develop.
3. Review the Resonator, weapon, Echo and skill modules.
4. Choose one achievable next upgrade instead of farming everything at once.
5. Use the material-source shortcut for the missing requirement.
6. Return after gathering it and check your progress.

Success check: You have a clear next upgrade and know its material source.

Troubleshooting: Recommendations differ from another account? The plan considers your highest SOL3 Phase. Preview character details missing? Some preview plans become available closer to release.
