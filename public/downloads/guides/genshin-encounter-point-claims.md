## Use banked Encounter Points for commissions

Genshin Impact · Daily commission reward system · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Daily commission reward system

Each 30 Original Resin used lets banked points supply one commission reward.

Before you start: Unlocked commissions and some Long-Term Encounter Points.

1. Open the commission page and check how many rewards remain today.
2. Read its question-mark help for the current point rules.
3. Check your Long-Term Encounter Point balance.
4. Use Original Resin on activities you already need.
5. Return after each 30 Resin used and claim available commission rewards.
6. Stop counting commission rewards after the daily total reaches four.

Success check: The commission reward counter increases after a claim.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Banked points do not vanish at the daily reset. Check in-game help for longer-term expiry rules.
