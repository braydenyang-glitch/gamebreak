## Make upgrade materials from the Terminal

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · Synthesis on the Go · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Terminal Synthesis documented in Version 2.0

Use Terminal → Synthesis and choose Purification or Conversion deliberately.

Before you start: Access to Synthesis and the ingredients shown by an available recipe.

1. Read the exact material and rarity needed on your upgrade screen.
2. Open the Terminal and select Synthesis.
3. Use Purification when the recipe combines materials across rarities.
4. Use Conversion only for a listed exchange within the same category and rarity.
5. Inspect the ingredients, quantity and resulting item before confirming.
6. Return to the upgrade screen and check your available amount.

Success check: Your inventory contains the intended upgrade material.

Troubleshooting: Recipe unavailable? Only listed recipes are supported; an arbitrary item cannot be converted. Still short? Recheck both rarity and required quantity before using more materials.

The menu and recipe shown in the current game take priority over old material lists.
