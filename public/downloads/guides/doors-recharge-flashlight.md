## Restore a drained flashlight

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · ordinary run without modifiers · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Hotel mechanics · community documentation

Carry the flashlight and pick up a compatible battery when its charge is low.

Before you start: A flashlight that is not fully charged and a battery pickup.

1. Check the flashlight’s charge indicator.
2. Keep it in your inventory while searching nearby containers and furniture.
3. Locate a battery pickup.
4. Collect it and watch the charge indicator increase.
5. Use the restored light to inspect the next dark route.

Success check: The flashlight gains charge and illuminates the route again.

Troubleshooting: Cannot collect the battery? Your compatible lights may already be full. No compatible light carried? Batteries cannot recharge a lighter.
