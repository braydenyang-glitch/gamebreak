## Unload a chest boat without breaking it

Minecraft · Java Edition 1.19+ · boats with chests · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Java Edition 1.19+ · boats with chests

Sneak and interact from outside to open its chest.

Before you start: An existing boat with a chest and a safe shoreline.

1. Bring the loaded boat to a safe shoreline.
2. While riding, open inventory to access its chest.
3. Move the items you need into free inventory slots.
4. From outside, sneak and interact with the boat to reopen storage.
5. Transfer remaining cargo before deciding whether to remove the boat.

Success check: Cargo is in your inventory or shore storage without breaking the loaded boat.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Java scope is intentional; controls and behavior on other editions are not verified here.
