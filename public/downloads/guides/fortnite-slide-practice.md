## Slide through a short exposed route

Fortnite · Battle Royale / Zero Build · sliding · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Sliding documented December 2021

Hold Crouch while running to start a slide.

Before you start: A clear route and a working Crouch binding.

1. Find a short, unobstructed route in a quiet area.
2. Identify cover or a safe stopping point at its end.
3. Start running, then hold your Crouch control.
4. Keep the camera facing the route while sliding.
5. Try aiming during a second attempt after the movement feels familiar.
6. Compare the slide with a normal run before choosing it in combat.

Success check: You can trigger a slide deliberately and keep track of your destination.

Troubleshooting: Only crouching? Begin running before holding the control. On flat ground? Sliding does not require a downhill slope.
