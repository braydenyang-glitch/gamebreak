Before you start: The relevant learned recipe, ingredients and access to cooking.

1. Decide whether you need Echo drops or ordinary enemy materials.
2. Inspect the recipe and effect before spending ingredients.
3. For Echo farming, check Sweet & Sour Pork or Lemon-Braised Pork.
4. For enemy materials, check Caltrop Stir Fry or Stuffed Tofu.
5. Prepare and use the selected dish before the relevant overworld fights.
6. Read its current duration and limits in the item description.

Success check: The active food effect matches the type of farming you planned.
