## Check stored Waveplate Crystals before farming

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · standard overworld and menus · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Waveplate Crystal storage documented in Version 1.2

Read both energy counts on the Map, then exchange stored Crystals if needed.

Before you start: Waveplate Crystals already stored and an available reward activity.

1. Open the Map and inspect the Waveplate and Waveplate Crystal counts.
2. Choose a challenge whose materials you actually need.
3. Compare its reward cost with your available Waveplates.
4. If short, use the energy replenishment prompts to exchange stored Crystals.
5. Check the new total before entering or claiming the reward.

Success check: You know whether your stored energy covers the planned claim.

Troubleshooting: No Crystals stored? They accumulate after normal Waveplates reach their cap. Not enough energy? Exchange only the stored amount you have; this is not unlimited energy.

Counts and the exchange mechanic were checked in archived publisher text. The live confirmation prompts determine available amounts.
