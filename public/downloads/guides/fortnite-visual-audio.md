## Turn on visual sound cues

Fortnite · Battle Royale · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Audio setting · menu untested

Open Settings → Audio, enable Visualize Sound Effects, and apply the change.

Before you start: Access to Settings in Fortnite.

1. From the lobby, open your profile menu and select the Settings gear.
2. Open the Audio tab and turn Visualize Sound Effects on.
3. Apply the setting, then check the cues during a match.

Success check: The setting remains enabled after Apply and relevant sound indicators appear during play.

Troubleshooting: If you cannot find it, check the Audio tab rather than Video or controller bindings.

Visual cues are an aid; they do not show every nearby player or replace all game sounds.

Sources: [Epic Games · Visualize Sound Effects](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/a25071786?lang=en-US)
