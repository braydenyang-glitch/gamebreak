Before you start: A mobile client and access to Graphics settings.

1. Open Settings → Graphics → Graphics Quality.
2. Start with the device’s recommended quality option.
3. Enable Auto FPS if you want automatic reductions under heavy load.
4. Test a short route that previously felt unstable.
5. Compare responsiveness and visibility before changing another option.

Success check: The settings suit the device better during the same short test.

Troubleshooting: Image quality drops during play? Auto FPS can lower quality and the frame-rate limit under overload. Still unstable? Lower the preset and retest; this feature is not a guaranteed fix.
