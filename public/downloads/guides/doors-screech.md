Before you start: A normal Hotel run and control of your camera.

1. In a dark Hotel room, listen for the quiet warning and watch for the screen cue.
2. Turn your camera to locate Screech, including above and below your view.
3. Look directly at it in time, then continue along your route.

Success check: Screech retreats without biting your character.

Troubleshooting: Keep scanning vertically as well as behind you; simply moving away does not address its warning.
