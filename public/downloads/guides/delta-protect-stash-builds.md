## Stop loadouts from dismantling stored weapons

Delta Force · PC Operations · loadout assembly settings · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC Operations · loadout assembly settings

Disable the option that lets loadouts dismantle stash weapons.

Before you start: A stored weapon build and a loadout to apply.

1. Inspect the stored weapon and note its attachments.
2. Open loadout assembly settings.
3. Find “Allow loadout to dismantle stash weapons”.
4. Turn that option off before applying another loadout.
5. Apply the loadout and recheck the protected stored weapon.

Success check: The setting is disabled and the stored build remains assembled.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.
