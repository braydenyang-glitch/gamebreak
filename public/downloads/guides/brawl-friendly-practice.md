## Practise a mode in a Friendly Battle

Brawl Stars · Friendly Battles · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Friendly Battles · event-dependent slots

Open the top-right menu, select Friendly Battle and choose the event you want to practise.

Before you start: Access to Friendly Battle and the desired event.

1. Open the hamburger menu and choose Friendly Battle.
2. Select the event type and invite available friends or Club members.
3. Use a slot's power button if you want to disable its bot.
4. Use the wrench menu to review match rules before starting.

Success check: A practice room uses your intended mode and participant settings.

Troubleshooting: Check the event's player limit if an invitation cannot be added.

Friendly Battles do not award trophies. Supercell describes fully upgraded Brawlers here; available player slots depend on the event.
