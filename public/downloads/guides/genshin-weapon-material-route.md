## Build a weapon-material farming plan

Genshin Impact · Weapon upgrades · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
General weapon-upgrade workflow

Check the exact missing material before choosing a farming activity.

Before you start: An owned weapon and access to its upgrade screen.

1. Open the weapon upgrade screen and note the missing materials and Mora.
2. For ascension drops, check the relevant Domain of Forgery and its daily schedule.
3. Use the Crafting Bench if you already own lower-rarity materials from the same family.
4. For enemy drops, track the required enemy with the Adventurer’s Handbook.
5. Prepare enhancement ore through the Blacksmith; use Expeditions to gather supporting resources.
6. If Mora is the blocker, choose a Blossom of Wealth, commissions or available quests. Return and review the upgrade cost.

Success check: Your selected upgrade has a clear source for every missing resource.

Troubleshooting: Wrong domain reward: Check today’s rotation before spending Resin. Enough enemy drops, but upgrade still blocked: Review the separate ore, ascension-material and Mora requirements.

Domain rotations and recipes should be read in your current client. This is a resource workflow, not a recommended weapon ranking.
