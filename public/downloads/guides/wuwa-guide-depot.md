Open Guide Depot from the Terminal, then select a Resonator to view build recommendations.

Before you start: Access to Guide Depot and the desired Resonator entry.

1. Open the Terminal and choose Guide Depot.
2. Select the Resonator you want to improve.
3. Review Echoes, weapon, upgrade priority and teammate recommendations.
4. If multiple guides are available, switch between them and compare their assumptions with your account.

Success check: You can identify an upgrade that fits the equipment you own.

Troubleshooting: Check the guide's assumptions and refresh status before spending scarce materials.

Kuro's Version2.4 notes explicitly warn that data refreshes may be delayed. Recommendations depend on their author; they are not a guarantee of the latest optimal build.
