## Send requested supplies to a teammate

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · team matches · radio requests · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Item sharing documented in PC Update 5.1

Have your teammate request supplies, then use Interact at their request icon.

Before you start: A nearby teammate and the requested spare item.

1. Move close together behind cover.
2. Ask the receiver to request healing or ammunition through radio messages.
3. Look for the request icon above their character.
4. Use the shown Interact prompt to throw the requested item.
5. Have the receiver check their inventory or the ground nearby.

Success check: The receiver obtains the requested supplies.

Troubleshooting: No delivery? Move closer and stop running; range and position can prevent the throw. Inventory unchanged? A full inventory sends the supplies to the ground.
