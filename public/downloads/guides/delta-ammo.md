## Check ammo before your Operations run

Delta Force · Operations · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-26. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Operations fundamentals · PC build untested

Compare the ammunition’s penetration level with the armour you expect. A strong gun alone does not guarantee effective damage.

Before you start: Access to your Operations loadout and ammunition details.

1. Inspect the ammunition that fits your equipped weapon.
2. Read the current penetration level and damage details. Do not assume that level 6 is the maximum; official patch notes also document level-7 penetration rounds.
3. Pack compatible spare ammunition and keep the loadout within your budget.

Success check: Your weapon, loaded rounds and spare ammunition are compatible.

Troubleshooting: If damage seems weak, inspect penetration and the target’s armour before replacing the whole weapon.

The source is Garena-published and includes mobile-specific sections. Compare ammunition and armour values in your Global PC client; regional codes and balance values may differ.
