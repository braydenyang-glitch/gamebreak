## Protect eligible loot in your Safe Box

Delta Force · Operations · Garena documentation · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Garena Operations · access and restrictions depend on season

In Operations, the Safe Box protects items placed inside it when you die. It does not protect your whole backpack.

Before you start: An active Safe Box and items accepted by its current rules.

1. Inspect your active Safe Box, its space and any access expiry.
2. Choose eligible valuables or mission items you most want to keep.
3. Place them inside the Safe Box and confirm their new location.
4. Recheck its contents during looting; plan an extraction for everything outside it.

Success check: Your chosen eligible items are visibly inside the Safe Box.

Troubleshooting: An item may be restricted or too large. Seasonal access can expire; check the displayed rules.

Based on Garena’s Operations documentation. Global service rules, item restrictions and seasonal access may differ.
