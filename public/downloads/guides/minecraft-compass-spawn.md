## Navigate back toward world spawn

Minecraft · Java / Bedrock · Overworld · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Ordinary compass; excludes lodestone and recovery compasses

Follow the red needle in the Overworld, while choosing a safe route around obstacles.

Before you start: Four iron ingots, one redstone dust and a crafting table.

1. Put redstone dust in the crafting grid’s centre.
2. Place an iron ingot above, below, left and right of it.
3. Take the ordinary compass and enter the Overworld.
4. Observe the red needle and turn toward its direction.
5. Travel safely in that direction, checking the needle as you move.
6. Use known landmarks near world spawn to locate your destination.

Success check: You move back toward the world’s spawn area.

Troubleshooting: Compass does not lead to my house: It targets world spawn; sleeping in a bed does not make it a home locator. Needle behaves strangely: A normal compass is not a reliable route guide in other dimensions.

This is not a pathfinding tool. A base away from spawn needs a separate navigation method.
