## Practice aiming and reloading first

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · Basic Training · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Update 42.2 documentation · PC build untested

Use Basic Training’s weapon chapter to practise ammo pickup, reloading and aiming down sights.

Before you start: PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS on PC with the documented training flow.

1. Enter Play → Training → Basic Training.
2. Progress to chapter 3, the weapon and ammunition lesson.
3. Follow the NPC and complete the aiming, reloading and firing checklist.

Success check: The weapon chapter checklist is complete.

Troubleshooting: If the chapter does not advance, look for an unfinished checklist item.

This flow is documented in Update 42.2. Check your installed build if the menu differs.
