## Join Polaris Patrol as an Adjutant

Delta Force · PC Operations · Garena Polaris Patrol event · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC Operations · Garena Polaris Patrol event

Heavy Guard and Comms Guard Adjutants do not require the badge.

Before you start: An active event and a squad containing Desmoulins.

1. Check that Polaris Patrol is active on your server.
2. Join a squad with a player eligible to deploy as Desmoulins.
3. Choose an available Heavy Guard or Comms Guard Adjutant role.
4. Review the role loadout and selected event map.
5. Deploy and follow the role’s To-Do objectives.
6. Review Performance Points after the run.

Success check: You enter the patrol with the intended Adjutant role.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Garena schedule: 16 Oct 2026 08:00 to 27 Jan 2027 10:00, UTC+8. Other regions may differ.
