During The Hotel chase, follow Guiding Light’s blue cues through the correct doors and crouch spaces.

Before you start: A Hotel Seek chase with movement controls ready.

1. As the chase begins, move forward immediately and look for the blue-lit route.
2. At a split, follow the blue guidance to the correct door; crouch where it marks a low opening.
3. In the final hallway, avoid the hands and burning chandeliers while moving to the exit.

Success check: You reach the end of the chase.

Troubleshooting: At a split, look for the blue cue instead of guessing a door.

This covers The Hotel chase only. Other floors and modifiers may have different hazards.
