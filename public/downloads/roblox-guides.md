## Survive Rush in DOORS

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-06. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
DOORS · build not playtested

When lights flicker, find a hiding spot. Listen for Rush getting close before hiding.

Before you start: The Hotel, without special modifiers; a usable hiding place.

1. In The Hotel, treat flickering lights as a warning and locate an empty hiding spot.
2. Listen for Rush approaching, then enter the hiding spot before it reaches you.
3. After Rush passes, leave the hiding spot. Staying hidden too long can trigger Hide.

Success check: Rush passes without hitting your character.

Troubleshooting: Separate flickering lights from the approaching sound so you do not hide too early.

This example covers ordinary Hotel encounters, not every modifier or special floor. Dark rooms can have different warning cues.

## Survive Ambush returning through the rooms

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · ordinary run without modifiers · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Hotel mechanics · community reference

Hide for the approaching pass, listen as it moves away, and be ready to hide again.

Before you start: An Ambush encounter, audible cues and a usable hiding place.

1. Recognize the approaching rushing sound and find a usable hiding place.
2. Enter as Ambush approaches your room.
3. Listen until it moves away before leaving the hiding place.
4. Stay beside that hiding place instead of opening more rooms.
5. Hide again when the returning sound approaches.
6. Repeat as needed; continue only after the encounter has ended.

Success check: You survive its passes and can continue through the Hotel.

Troubleshooting: Hit on the return? Stay ready after the first pass; Ambush can rebound. Timing feels different? Use sound, not a fixed bounce count or countdown.

## Choose the real door when Dupe appears

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · ordinary run without modifiers · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Hotel mechanics · community reference

Remember the last door number and choose the next number in sequence.

Before you start: A regular Hotel run with door labels visible at entry.

1. Read the number each time you pass through a numbered door.
2. When several exits appear, stop before approaching a lock.
3. Work out the next number from the door you just used.
4. Choose the matching exit; do not test a suspicious lock.
5. Listen cautiously for the rumble from a false door if uncertain.
6. Update your remembered number after entering the next room.

Success check: You progress through the correct exit without a Dupe attack.

Troubleshooting: Labels scrambled? The key label can scramble too; it is not a guaranteed backup. No rumbling? Modifiers can remove that cue; this guide excludes those runs.

No fixed door offset or guessing trick is assumed.

## Solve the Library’s symbol code

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · ordinary run without modifiers · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Hotel mechanics · community documentation

Match book symbols to digits, then order them using the Solution Paper.

Before you start: The Library encounter and its collected puzzle clues.

1. Enter cautiously and watch Figure’s patrol before crossing its route.
2. Collect marked books from the shelves; note each symbol and number.
3. Collect the Solution Paper from the office desk.
4. Read its five symbol positions in Roman-numeral order.
5. Replace each symbol with the number recorded from its book.
6. Enter that five-digit code at the upstairs exit and leave promptly.

Success check: The Library exit unlocks with the code from your current run.

Troubleshooting: A digit is missing? Search for the book with that symbol. Code rejected? Recheck the paper’s order; a code from another run will not help.

## Power the Hotel’s exit elevator

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · ordinary run without modifiers · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Hotel mechanics · community documentation

Collect the breaker pieces, solve the displayed switch sequence, then reach the elevator.

Before you start: Door 100 and access to a usable hiding place.

1. Locate a hiding place before activating the gate lever.
2. Pull the lever and hide from Figure’s opening chase.
3. After the chase, search the storage area carefully for all ten breaker pieces.
4. Get the Electrical Room Key upstairs, or use a compatible lockpick on the breaker-room door.
5. Place the pieces in the circuit breaker.
6. For each displayed number, set its switch on for a filled square and off for an empty square.
7. For the final mystery number, identify the switch number missing from that sequence.
8. Once power returns, head for the elevator immediately.

Success check: The elevator is powered and you reach the Hotel exit.

Troubleshooting: Puzzle will not begin? Check that every breaker piece has been inserted. Figure returns? Do not remain at the panel after solving it.

## Find controls and reduce distracting motion

Roblox · DOORS · Roblox platform · DOORS preparation · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Roblox platform help · experience support varies

Open the in-experience menu: Help explains device controls; Settings includes Reduce Motion and background contrast options.

Before you start: An active Roblox experience and access to its menu.

1. Open the Roblox menu while in the experience.
2. Read Help for keyboard, mouse or touch instructions.
3. In Settings, enable Reduce Motion if you prefer fewer supported effects.
4. Adjust Background Transparency for more readable supported interface backgrounds.
5. Return to the experience and compare the result.

Success check: You can find the relevant controls and judge whether the supported effects are more comfortable.

Troubleshooting: Developers can lock camera behavior. Roblox settings do not guarantee control of every custom DOORS effect.

This is platform help, not a claim that DOORS supports every optional camera mode or accessibility setting.
