# GameBreak — Genshin Impact

Genshin Impact combines open-world exploration with a party you can switch during action combat. Elemental Skills, Bursts and reactions connect your characters. Follow the story, open travel points and improve a small working team before chasing every collectible or perfect Artifact.

## Start with one clear elemental reaction

Learn what your own attacks apply before copying a long rotation.

General elemental combat; no damage benchmark or current-meta claim.

1. Read the Skill and Burst descriptions of two characters; identify which action actually applies an element.
2. On a suitable enemy, apply Hydro, Pyro, Cryo or Electro, then use an Anemo attack to observe Swirl.
3. Watch the elemental icon and reaction name. Repeat slowly so you can tell which action caused the reaction.
4. Add movement and a defensive option before extending the sequence. Save a little stamina for dodging.

Enemy immunities and character-specific mechanics can change the result; this is a beginner drill, not a universal rotation.

## Build a party with a job for every slot

A usable team needs a simple sequence and a way to stay alive.

Early team-building workflow; equipment recommendations remain character-specific.

1. Choose one main way to deal damage using a character you own; write down its key Skill or Burst.
2. Choose teammates that help that action through reactions, buffs, healing or shielding. This is a planning template, not a fixed composition.
3. Check weapon level, relevant Talents and useful Artifact main stats before investing in an entirely new party.
4. Try the same encounter twice. Change one weak point—survival, Energy or damage—instead of replacing everything at once.

A five-star item or a popular team does not automatically fit your available characters and upgrades.

## Spend Resin toward a visible upgrade

Plan from the missing material rather than farming without a target.

Material planning; costs and reward multipliers come from the live client.

1. Open the next character or weapon upgrade and list its missing materials and Mora.
2. Use the material’s source panel; check the displayed Domain schedule before travelling to a weapon-material challenge.
3. Use regenerating Original Resin for the reward that removes your current bottleneck; check the claim cost before confirming.
4. After the upgrade is ready, stop that farm and set the next target. Keep Fragile Resin separate from your everyday plan.

Do not assume premium-currency refills are necessary; this plan works with free regeneration.

## Explore one area without losing your place

Combine travel points, quest tracking and map layers.

Spoiler-light exploration workflow, not a complete collectible route.

1. Activate nearby Waypoints and Statues, then track one quest or collectible goal instead of several distant ones.
2. For a missing Oculus, compare the official Teyvat Interactive Map with your area and underground layer.
3. If a Resonance Stone marks an area, search above, below and behind nearby terrain; its circle is not an exact pin.
4. Mark confirmed finds and save unfinished puzzles for later. Check stamina before a long climb or swim back.

Some collectibles require a quest or regional mechanic; a visible map marker does not prove the route is already open.

## Read a Wish before using currency

Separate the banner, its rules and your own spending limit.

Rules-reading and budgeting; no pull-rate forecast or banner recommendation.

1. Open the exact Wish you intend to use and read Details, including its featured items and end time.
2. Check whether the button consumes Intertwined Fate, Acquaint Fate or another stated resource before converting Primogems.
3. Review the history and guarantee rules for that Wish type. Do not combine character, weapon and special-banner assumptions.
4. Set a maximum currency amount, try any available character trial, and stop at your chosen limit.

A guarantee for a rarity is not always a guarantee for your chosen item; current in-game Details take priority.

## When Adventure Rank stops increasing

Separate account progression from character and weapon upgrades.

General rank troubleshooting; use the exact unlock conditions shown in your account.

1. Check your Adventure Rank and the Quest Menu for an Adventure Rank Ascension task when EXP appears capped.
2. Before the challenge, review your active team’s weapon levels, important Talents, Artifacts and survival option.
3. Complete the required ascension task when ready. Stored Adventure EXP is applied after the required quest is cleared.
4. If overworld difficulty becomes uncomfortable, inspect the available World Level adjustment and improve one bottleneck before retrying.

Account rank is not a substitute for upgraded equipment; raising difficulty can expose an unfinished team.
