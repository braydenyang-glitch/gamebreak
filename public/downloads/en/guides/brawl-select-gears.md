## Choose a Gear for the situation

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · Brawler equipment · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher equipment help

Choose an owned Gear whose condition matches how you expect to play.

Before you start: A Brawler with an unlocked Gear slot and an owned compatible Gear.

1. Open the Brawler you intend to use.
2. Check whether a Gear slot is unlocked.
3. Compare available Gear descriptions and their activation conditions.
4. Select an owned Gear that fits the intended map or plan.
5. Play while deliberately checking that condition, such as moving through bushes for Speed.
6. After the match, reconsider whether that condition occurred often enough.

Success check: The Gear is equipped and you understand when its passive bonus applies.

Troubleshooting: Slot locked? Check the required Power Level shown in-game. Bonus seems absent? Some Gears require a specific health, movement or terrain condition. Gear missing? Some rarities are available only to particular Brawlers.

This is a comparison method, not a best-build ranking. Exact bonuses can change.
