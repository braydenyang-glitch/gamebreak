## Locate supplies before entering Zero Dam

Delta Force · Operations · Zero Dam · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC Operations · current build not tested

Open Delta Force’s map tool, choose Zero Dam, then turn on the supply and extraction filters.

Before you start: Internet for the official map tool; the intended map and difficulty.

1. Open the official map tool and select the Zero Dam map and difficulty you intend to play.
2. Turn on the supply markers you need, such as Med Kit or Safe, along with extraction points.
3. Choose a nearby supply area and a possible exit; recheck conditions in your current match.

Success check: You can identify a supply area and a possible exit.

Troubleshooting: Clear other map filters if markers are hard to see. Recheck the current match.

The map is a planning aid. Loot and exit availability can change between matches and updates.
