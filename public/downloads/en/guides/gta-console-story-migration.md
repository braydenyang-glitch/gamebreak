## Move a Story Mode save to the newer console version

Grand Theft Auto V · PS4 / Xbox One → PS5 / Xbox Series X|S · Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PS4 / Xbox One → PS5 / Xbox Series X|S

Upload from the old version, then download in the new version.

Before you start: Linked console accounts and the same Rockstar Games account.

1. Confirm both console accounts are linked to the intended Rockstar account.
2. On PS4 or Xbox One, open Pause → Game → Upload Save Game.
3. Select the intended Story save and confirm the upload.
4. On PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, choose Download Save Game from the Story page or Game menu.
5. Review the selected save before accepting migration.
6. Check the loaded progress; future saves on each version are separate.

Success check: The selected Story progress loads in the newer console version.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This does not migrate PC, PS3 or Xbox 360 saves. Read the confirmation carefully.
