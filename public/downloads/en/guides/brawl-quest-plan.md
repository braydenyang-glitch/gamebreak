## Choose a quest that fits your next match

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · standard client · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher support checked 20 October 2026

Read a quest, select its required Brawler or mode, then check progress afterward.

Before you start: Quests unlocked and an available quest.

1. Open the Quest button or the Quests tab in Brawl Pass.
2. Read the full requirement and its expiry.
3. Tap a Brawler-specific quest to select that Brawler automatically.
4. Choose a matching mode and play toward the stated requirement.
5. Return to the quest view and check progress.
6. If unsuitable, consider an available reroll before starting another match.

Success check: The intended quest records progress from your match.

Troubleshooting: No progress? Recheck the exact Brawler, mode and task wording. No reroll left? Rerolls are limited; select another suitable quest instead.
