## Track a Resonator's ascension materials

Wuthering Waves · Open-world material tracking · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Collection Spots · introduced in1.3

Select an ascension material in Backpack or the Resonators screen, then open Collection Spots.

Before you start: A supported ascension material and access to its region.

1. Find the material needed for your planned ascension.
2. Select its icon in Backpack or the Resonators screen.
3. Choose Collection Spots to show a location on the map.
4. Collect the material there; after that spot is exhausted, the next location becomes trackable.

Success check: The map shows a material collection location.

Troubleshooting: Check region access and the item's available source options if Collection Spots is unavailable.

Official Version1.3 notes document this feature. Tracking locations is not a promise that all materials are available immediately or that every region is unlocked.
