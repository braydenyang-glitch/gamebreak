## Find a missing Seelie

Genshin Impact · Traditional Seelie courts · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-06. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Patch not playtested

Use Elemental Sight next to an empty Seelie court to look for a direction clue.

Before you start: An empty traditional Seelie court.

1. Find an empty Seelie court in an area with this mechanic.
2. Activate Elemental Sight and look for the trail showing the Seelie’s general direction.
3. Search that area, then follow the Seelie back to its court.

Success check: The Seelie returns to the court.

Troubleshooting: Check whether this is a regional puzzle with different rules.

This does not apply to every regional puzzle or special Seelie mechanic.
