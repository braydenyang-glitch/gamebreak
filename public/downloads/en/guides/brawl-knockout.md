## Play the objective in Knockout

Brawl Stars · Knockout · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Knockout · current build not tested

Win the best-of-three rounds by eliminating the other team; defeated Brawlers stay out until the next round.

Before you start: Knockout available in mode selection.

1. Select Knockout when it is available in the rotating game modes.
2. Play with your team and protect surviving players; a defeated Brawler cannot return in that round.
3. Watch the shrinking poison clouds if a round runs long. Win two rounds to take the match.

Success check: Your team wins two rounds.
