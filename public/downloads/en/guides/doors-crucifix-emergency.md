## Use a Crucifix without assuming invincibility

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · ordinary run without modifiers · PC / Mobile

Before you start: A Crucifix and an escape route.

1. Identify the approaching threat and your normal escape option.
2. Equip the Crucifix before the encounter reaches you.
3. Let its automatic effect trigger on a supported entity.
4. If the chains turn red, keep escaping: the entity is breaking free.
5. Check your inventory before relying on it again.

Success check: You survive the interruption and continue moving safely.

Troubleshooting: Seek or Figure still active? The Crucifix only delays those bosses. No protection from Snare? The item does not counter every threat.
