## Add your own music to Self Radio

Grand Theft Auto V · PC Legacy · Self Radio · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC Legacy · Self Radio

Copy music to User Music, then run the audio scan.

Before you start: Supported non-DRM music files on your PC.

1. Open Documents/Rockstar Games/GTAV/User Music.
2. Copy your supported MP3, WMA or M4A files there.
3. Open GTA V and go to Pause → Settings → Audio.
4. Run Quick Scan or Full Scan for music.
5. Select Self Radio in a vehicle.
6. Choose Sequential or Shuffle if you want track skipping.

Success check: Self Radio plays a scanned local track.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

The documented folder applies to the original PC layout; an Enhanced-specific folder is not asserted.
