## Retry a stuck domain without abandoning available rewards

Genshin Impact · Quest or challenge domain troubleshooting · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Quest or challenge domain troubleshooting

Claim any available completion loot before leaving.

Before you start: A domain whose objective or challenge appears stuck.

1. Read the active objective and check whether the challenge is already complete.
2. Look for available end rewards.
3. Claim those rewards before exiting.
4. Leave the domain, then enter it again.
5. Repeat the objective and check whether progress resumes.

Success check: The objective resumes after re-entry, or you have clear evidence for Support.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Re-entry is a documented troubleshooting attempt, not a guaranteed repair.
