## Break an enemy’s Toughness

Honkai: Star Rail · Standard combat · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Patch not playtested

Attack with a damage type matching an enemy’s weakness to lower its Toughness bar.

Before you start: A matching damage type available in your party.

1. Check the weakness symbols displayed above the enemy.
2. Use a character whose attack matches one of those types.
3. Keep reducing the white Toughness bar until Weakness Break triggers.

Success check: The enemy’s white Toughness bar decreases.

Troubleshooting: Inspect battle effects if the enemy’s Toughness is protected.

Some enemies and abilities change how breaking works. Check the battle’s effect descriptions.
