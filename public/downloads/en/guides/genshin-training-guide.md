## Plan your next character upgrade

Genshin Impact · Character progression · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Training Guide · feature documented in5.3

Open a character, then use the Training Guide shortcut to review upgrade suggestions.

Before you start: Training Guide available on your account.

1. Open the character you want to improve.
2. Use the Training Guide shortcut on the character interface.
3. Compare the character, weapon and artifact sections before spending materials.
4. Choose one useful upgrade, then check its material requirements in-game.

Success check: You can identify one specific upgrade and its required materials.

Troubleshooting: If the shortcut is missing, check account progress and your installed version.

The shortcut is documented in Version5.3. Suggestions are a starting point, not a universal best build or a current endgame ranking.
