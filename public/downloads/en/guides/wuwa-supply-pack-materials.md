## Use a Supply Pack for a missing material

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · standard overworld and menus · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Supply Pack shortcut documented in Version 2.2

From a missing material’s How to Get menu, select Supply Pack.

Before you start: A leveling requirement and a matching Supply Pack in the Backpack.

1. Open the upgrade you want to complete.
2. Select its missing material and open How to Get.
3. Choose Supply Pack if a matching pack is available.
4. Review the material and amount before opening the pack.
5. Return to the upgrade and check the remaining requirements.

Success check: The needed material is available for the planned upgrade.

Troubleshooting: Supply Pack option absent? You may not own a pack containing that material. Different pack used first? The documented system prioritizes packs with fewer available choices.
