## Move compatible attachments automatically

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · weapon attachments · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher auto-attachment settings help

Review the auto-attachment options in Settings → Gameplay.

Before you start: Two weapons and compatible attachments, preferably in Training Mode.

1. Open Settings → Gameplay and locate the auto-attachment options.
2. Enable the pickup or weapon-replacement behavior you want.
3. Equip an attachment on your current weapon.
4. Replace that weapon with a compatible one on the ground.
5. Inspect the new weapon’s slots before continuing.

Success check: Compatible attachments move to the replacement weapon.

Troubleshooting: Some stayed in inventory? The new weapon may not support them. Pickup does not equip? Check the option for your pickup method and whether the slot is already occupied.
