## Set flying controls without a number pad

Grand Theft Auto V · PC · keyboard and mouse flying · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC · keyboard and mouse flying

Rebind flight keys or choose mouse flying.

Before you start: A PC keyboard and access to control settings.

1. Open settings and inspect the aircraft bindings.
2. Rebind number-pad actions to keys you can reach.
3. Alternatively review the mouse-flying option under Camera → Vehicle.
4. Adjust mouse sensitivity and auto-centering if using the mouse.
5. Test turning and climbing in a low-pressure flight before the mission.

Success check: You can operate the required flight actions with your chosen controls.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

The practice sequence is editorial. This does not promise a mission pass.
