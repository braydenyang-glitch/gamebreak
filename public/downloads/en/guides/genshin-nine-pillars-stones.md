## Activate the Nine Pillars with Stones of Remembrance

Genshin Impact · Liyue · Nine Pillars of Peace activation · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Liyue · Nine Pillars of Peace activation

The stone interaction is on each pillar’s carved face.

Before you start: Nine Stones of Remembrance from fully upgrading Liyue Statues.

1. Check Liyue Statue upgrades and your Stone of Remembrance count.
2. Locate the nine pillars on the quest map.
3. Approach a pillar that still needs a stone.
4. Face its carving and use the interaction prompt.
5. Repeat for the remaining pillars, then follow the updated objective.

Success check: All nine stones are inserted and the quest objective advances.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This guide ends at pillar activation; the later combat and reward choice are separate.
