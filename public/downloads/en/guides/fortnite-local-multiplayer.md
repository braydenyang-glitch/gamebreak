## Start a two-player split-screen match

Fortnite · Battle Royale / Zero Build · supported PlayStation and Xbox consoles · Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher local-multiplayer compatibility help

Connect the second controller in the lobby and follow the second-player sign-in prompts.

Before you start: Two controllers, separate player accounts, the same language and stable internet.

1. Launch Fortnite and enter the lobby.
2. Connect the second controller.
3. Choose the second player’s console account; guest or Quick Play accounts do not work.
4. Follow the on-screen Epic account linking or sign-in prompts.
5. Check that both players appear in the lobby.
6. Choose a supported local-multiplayer Duos or Squads playlist and start.

Success check: The match displays both players on split screen.

Troubleshooting: Second screen missing in menus? Split screen appears during the match, not every submenu. Unsupported playlist or device? This feature excludes PC, mobile, Switch, Solos and Ranked.

If either player leaves or disconnects, the split-screen session ends.
