## Refuel at a Deston fuel pump

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · Deston · playlists with fuel pumps · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Deston fuel pumps documented in Update 18.2

Stop beside a usable pump and hold the driver’s Interact control.

Before you start: A vehicle missing fuel, a working pump and the driver’s seat.

1. Find an undamaged pump and check the surrounding area.
2. Drive close enough for its interaction prompt.
3. Bring the vehicle to a complete stop.
4. Remain in the driver’s seat and hold Interact to start refueling.
5. Watch the fuel level, then leave when finished.

Success check: The fuel gauge increases while the vehicle stays still.

Troubleshooting: Refueling cancels? Moving or changing seats interrupts it. Pump sparking or destroyed? Leave the area; damaged pumps can explode and destroyed ones cannot refuel.
