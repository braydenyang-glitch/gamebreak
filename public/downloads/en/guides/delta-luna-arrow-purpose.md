Delta Force · PC Operations · Luna gadgets · PC

PC Operations · Luna gadgets

Detection Arrow scans moving targets; Volt Arrow creates electrical damage.

Before you start: Luna unlocked and her current gadget descriptions.

1. Read both arrow descriptions in Luna’s current loadout.
2. Choose Detection Arrow when checking a route for movement.
3. Aim its flight path through the area you need information about.
4. Read the scan result before advancing.
5. Use Volt Arrow when you want a damaging contact area instead.
6. Reassess after enemies move; an earlier scan is not permanent knowledge.
