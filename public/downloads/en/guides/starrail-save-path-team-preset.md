## Save a team preset with the correct Path

Honkai: Star Rail · Team Lineup · multi-Path characters · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Team Lineup · multi-Path characters

A saved preset records the character’s current Path.

Before you start: A multi-Path character and access to Team Presets.

1. Open Team Lineup and select the intended team.
2. Switch the multi-Path character to the needed Path there.
3. Check the other team members and equipment.
4. Save the lineup as a Team Preset.
5. Recall the preset and confirm the saved Path appears.

Success check: The recalled team uses its saved Path.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This documents preset behavior, not a recommended team composition.
