## Use an Ultimate between turns

Honkai: Star Rail · Standard combat · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Energy-based Ultimates · build untested

When an Energy-based Ultimate is ready, select it to queue an extra action without waiting for that character’s normal turn.

Before you start: A character with an Energy-based Ultimate in a normal battle.

1. Watch the character’s Energy indicator during battle.
2. When the Ultimate becomes available, select it and choose a target if asked.
3. Confirm the action. The current animation may finish before your Ultimate starts.

Success check: The Ultimate executes and consumes its required resource.

Troubleshooting: If the button is unavailable, check the resource meter and any control effects on the character.

Some characters use special resources or have additional restrictions. Read the character’s current ability text.
