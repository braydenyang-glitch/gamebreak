## Build and test a short minecart route

Minecraft · Java / Bedrock · passenger minecart · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Java / Bedrock · passenger minecart

A minecart needs rails; uphill routes need propulsion.

Before you start: Five iron ingots, rails and a short prepared route.

1. Craft a minecart with five iron ingots in a U shape.
2. Lay a short connected rail route on prepared ground.
3. Place the minecart on a rail.
4. Use the cart to board and test the route.
5. Add powered rails where uphill movement needs help.
6. Use sneak to leave the cart after the test.

Success check: You can board, travel along the test track and exit.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This covers a basic passenger cart, not furnace-minecart behavior or a full rail station.
