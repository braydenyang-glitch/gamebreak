## Use a Gear Ticket for a prepared kit

A Gear Ticket supplies a kit including protection, weapons, ammunition and medical items.

Before you start: An available eligible Gear Ticket.

1. Check whether a Gear Ticket is available in your client.
2. Inspect its supplied kit and any displayed restrictions.
3. Confirm the ammunition and medical items before entering Operations.

Success check: You understand the kit before entering.

Troubleshooting: Check ticket requirements if it cannot be selected.
