## Use gyro for small aiming corrections

Fortnite · Battle Royale · compatible gyro hardware; excludes Xbox controllers and GeForce NOW · PC / Console / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Gyro feature guide published February 2022

Enable Gyro Aiming in Touch and Motion settings.

Before you start: Compatible PlayStation, Switch, PC controller or Android motion controls.

1. Open Settings → Touch and Motion.
2. Turn Gyro Aiming on.
3. Begin with an aiming-only Active Mode instead of Always.
4. Keep Flick Stick off while learning basic gyro.
5. Move the camera normally, then use small device movements for fine corrections.
6. Adjust sensitivity gradually after a short practice session.

Success check: You make small aim adjustments without large controller movements.

Troubleshooting: Nothing happens? Confirm the hardware supports gyro and your selected Active Mode is engaged. Aim assist feels different? Epic states it is disabled while gyro is active.
