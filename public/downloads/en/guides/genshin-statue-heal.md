## A Statue of The Seven won’t heal you?

Genshin Impact · Open-world exploration · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Exploration mechanic · build untested

Check the statue’s healing reserve, recovery percentage and individual character settings.

Before you start: An activated statue and a character below full HP.

1. Return to single-player to rule out Co-Op recovery restrictions.
2. Interact with an activated statue and inspect its recovery settings.
3. Check both the available healing reserve and each character’s recovery setting.

Success check: The selected character’s HP increases.

Troubleshooting: If the reserve is empty, check again later. For a single affected character, inspect its individual setting.

Co-Op healing requires Auto-Recover to be enabled. Healing reserves, percentage settings and the selected character can affect recovery.
