## Cross water without exhausting oxygen

Delta Force · Operations · swimming · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Swimming rules documented in Season Break

Watch oxygen underwater; sprint swimming spends resources faster.

Before you start: A map with water and a planned exit point.

1. Identify a nearby shore or place where you can surface.
2. Enter the water and use slower movement when conserving resources.
3. Watch oxygen while diving; do not wait until it runs out.
4. Resurface to restore oxygen.
5. Use weapons only from the surface; they cannot fire underwater.
6. After the match, inspect the Black Site Diving Center for swimming-related upgrades.

Success check: You reach your exit without losing HP to depleted oxygen.

Troubleshooting: HP drains underwater: Surface: empty oxygen causes health loss. Gun will not fire: Underwater weapon fire is unavailable; only certain gadgets work there.

Sprinting drains Stamina and oxygen faster. Garena’s July 2025 documentation is the source; compare your current service.
