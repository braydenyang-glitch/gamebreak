Use the map to identify an extraction point and check its requirements before heading there.

Before you start: Your current Operations map and enough time to move.

1. Open the current Operations map and identify its extraction points.
2. Read the selected point’s requirements. Do not assume every exit is always available.
3. Choose a route and leave enough time to reach the exit. Follow its activation instructions.

Success check: The match confirms a completed extraction.

Troubleshooting: An icon alone is insufficient; inspect the exit’s requirements and availability.
