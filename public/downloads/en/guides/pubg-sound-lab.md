## Learn gunshot direction in the Sound Lab

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · Training Mode Sound Lab · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Sound Lab · records added in Update 24.2

Enter the Aim/Sound Lab in Training Mode and choose sound practice.

Before you start: Training Mode and functioning game audio.

1. Enter Training Mode and find the Aim/Sound Lab access point.
2. Interact with a Lab helipad and choose sound practice.
3. Listen to the shot before choosing a target.
4. Shoot the target you think matches its direction.
5. Compare the displayed result with your guess.
6. Repeat and review your training record to track progress.

Success check: You finish a sound session and can compare its result with another attempt.

Troubleshooting: Aim targets instead? Return to mode selection and select sound practice. Entry blocked during 1v1 matchmaking? Cancel that queue before entering the Lab.

Use a comfortable listening volume. This practice does not guarantee detection of every live-match sound.
