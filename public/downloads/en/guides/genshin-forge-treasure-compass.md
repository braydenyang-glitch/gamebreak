## Forge a Treasure Compass at the Blacksmith

Genshin Impact · Unlocked regional compass blueprint · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Unlocked regional compass blueprint

Treasure Compasses are forged, not made at the Alchemy Bench.

Before you start: The relevant compass blueprint and its listed materials.

1. Check whether the regional blueprint is still in your inventory.
2. Use it once to learn the recipe if needed.
3. Visit a Blacksmith and open Forge.
4. Choose the matching regional Treasure Compass.
5. Gather any materials marked missing, then forge it.
6. Check your inventory for the finished gadget.

Success check: The compass appears in your gadget inventory.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This covers crafting after blueprint unlock, not every region’s reputation requirements.
