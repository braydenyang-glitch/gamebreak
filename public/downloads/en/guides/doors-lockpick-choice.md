## Spend a lockpick on a compatible lock

Roblox · DOORS · DOORS · The Hotel · ordinary run without modifiers · PC / Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Hotel mechanics · community documentation

Equip a lockpick and interact with an ordinary locked door or container.

Before you start: A lockpick and an eligible lock.

1. Inspect the lock before using the item.
2. Choose whether saving the key-search time is worth spending a lockpick.
3. Equip the lockpick from your inventory.
4. Use the lock’s interaction prompt and let the opening finish.
5. Check your remaining lockpicks before continuing.

Success check: The eligible lock opens without its normal key.

Troubleshooting: Library padlock or skeleton door? Those require their own mechanics. Item gone after opening? Lockpicks are consumed when used.
