## Remove mistaken discard flags from relics

Honkai: Star Rail · Relic inventory · Batch Undiscard · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Relic inventory · Batch Undiscard

Batch Undiscard clears flags; it does not recover salvaged items.

Before you start: Owned relics that are incorrectly marked for discard.

1. Open the relic inventory and identify the wrongly marked items.
2. Use filters to narrow the list if needed.
3. Inspect each selected relic’s stats and equipment status.
4. Use Batch Undiscard on the selected group.
5. Check that their discard markers are gone before any salvage action.

Success check: The selected relics no longer have discard flags.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.
