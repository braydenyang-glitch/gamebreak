## Understand a Brawler Blast unlock

Brawl Stars · Brawl Stars · standard client · Mobile

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Publisher support checked 20 October 2026

Collect enough Credits, complete the gallery, then inspect the unlocked Brawler.

Before you start: Enough Credits for the next available Brawler unlock.

1. Open the Brawler unlock area and check the Credit requirement.
2. When Brawler Blast activates, review the displayed targets.
3. Complete the shooting-gallery prompts.
4. If a choice appears, compare its offered Brawlers before selecting.
5. Inspect the final unlocked Brawler and its bonus upgrades.
6. Check the Power Level needed before trying a rewarded ability in battle.

Success check: You know which Brawler and usable upgrades the unlock provided.

Troubleshooting: Some targets are blank? Fewer remaining Brawlers of that rarity can cause this. Rewarded ability unavailable? Receiving it does not remove its Power Level requirement.
