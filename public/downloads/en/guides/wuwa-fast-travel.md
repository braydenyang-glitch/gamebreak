## Use a Resonance Beacon to fast travel

Wuthering Waves · Open-world exploration · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Open-world beacon mechanic · build untested

Activate a Resonance Beacon in person, then select the activated beacon on your map to travel back.

Before you start: Access to the open world and an activated beacon.

1. Reach a Resonance Beacon and interact with it to activate it.
2. Open the map and select an activated beacon.
3. Choose fast travel when available and wait for the destination to load.

Success check: Your character arrives at the beacon selected on the map.

Troubleshooting: If travel is unavailable, check that the beacon is activated and finish any restricted activity.

Story sequences and special activities can restrict travel. An unactivated beacon is not a travel destination.
