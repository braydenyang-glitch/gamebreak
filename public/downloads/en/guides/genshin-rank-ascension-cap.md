## Unblock Adventure Rank at 45 or 50

Genshin Impact · Adventure Rank 45 / 50 ascension quests · PC / Mobile / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Adventure Rank 45 / 50 ascension quests

Stored Adventure EXP is applied after the required ascension.

Before you start: Adventure Rank 45 or 50 with the corresponding ascension quest.

1. Confirm whether your Adventure Rank is 45 or 50.
2. Open the quest list and locate the ascension quest.
3. Use Ascension 3 at rank 45 or Ascension 4 at rank 50.
4. Follow its “Ascend: Clear the Ruins” objective.
5. Complete the challenge and check your rank and stored EXP.

Success check: The rank cap lifts after the matching quest is completed.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Only the documented rank 45 and 50 cases are covered.
