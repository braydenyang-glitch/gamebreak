Before you start: Condensed Resin recipe and an eligible reward screen.

2. Use Condensed Resin only when the reward screen offers it.
3. Treat the reward as three runs' quantity, not an increased individual drop rate.

Success check: You understand the cost and reward quantity before spending resin.
