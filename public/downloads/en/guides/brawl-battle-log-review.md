Publisher replay help

Use the main-screen menu → Battle Log to inspect a recent match.

Before you start: A recent match in Battle Log; replay eligibility for video review.

1. Tap the top-right menu on the main screen.
2. Choose the Battle Log clipboard icon.
3. Select the match you want to inspect.
4. Check its mode, map and team composition.
5. Open Replay if that battle meets the replay requirement.
6. Review one lost objective or elimination and choose one adjustment for the next match.

Success check: You identify the match and one action to improve.

Troubleshooting: Replay missing? Supercell lists a Brawler-trophy requirement, with separate Ranked eligibility. Old game missing? Battle Log contains a limited recent-match history.

Reviewing a mistake is a suggested exercise. Replay access is not guaranteed for every account or battle.
