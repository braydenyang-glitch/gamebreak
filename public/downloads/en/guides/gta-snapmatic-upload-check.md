## Upload a selected Snapmatic photo

Grand Theft Auto V · PS4 / Xbox One / PC · Gallery · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PS4 / Xbox One / PC · Gallery

Photos are not automatically uploaded.

Before you start: An existing Snapmatic photo and an online Rockstar connection.

1. Open Pause and move to the Gallery tab.
2. Highlight the photo you actually want to share.
3. Review it before publishing it to your Rockstar account.
4. Press R3 on PS4, right-stick click on Xbox One, or Left Ctrl on PC.
5. Wait and check the thumbnail’s lower-right corner for the small R* mark.

Success check: The thumbnail shows the mark indicating a successful upload.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This explains an explicit upload action; it does not automatically share any player content.
