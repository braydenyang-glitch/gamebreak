## Fix “Mission Disrupted” in Story Mode

Grand Theft Auto V · Story mode · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Story mode · current patch not tested

Leave the mission area until its marker returns, then take a taxi to the mission and skip the ride.

Before you start: Story Mode showing the specific “Mission Disrupted” message.

1. Drive away from the mission area until its map marker returns.
2. Call Downtown Cab Company and enter the taxi.
3. Choose the mission as the destination and skip the taxi trip. Try the mission again.

Success check: The mission starts after returning by taxi.

Troubleshooting: Keep the older save if the workaround fails.

Rockstar describes this workaround for the “Mission Disrupted” message. Keep an older save before troubleshooting.
