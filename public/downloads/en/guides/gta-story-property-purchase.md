## Find the correct place to buy a Story property

Grand Theft Auto V · GTA V · Story Mode property access · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
GTA V · Story Mode property access

Story Mode property purchases start at the location’s realty sign.

Before you start: A purchasable property shown on the Story Mode map.

1. Open the pause-menu map in Story Mode.
2. Find a house-shaped icon containing a dollar symbol.
3. Travel to that property.
4. Walk up to its realty sign.
5. Read the purchase prompt and price before deciding whether to buy.

Success check: The property’s own purchase prompt is visible.

Troubleshooting: Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

This locates the purchase interface; it does not list every unlock, character restriction or return on investment.
