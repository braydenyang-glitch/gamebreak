## Read the blue zone before moving

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC · patch not playtested

Look for the white circle on the map: it marks the next safe area before the blue zone closes.

Before you start: A live PC Battle Royale match and the map.

1. Open your map and locate your position relative to the white circle.
2. Check the phase timer and plan a route into the safe area.
3. Move before the blue zone catches you. Check the map again as the safe area changes.

Success check: You reach the safe area before the zone overtakes your route.

Troubleshooting: Recheck the white circle when the phase changes; your previous destination may no longer be safe.

This guide covers PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS on PC. It is not a PUBG Mobile guide.

## Move a knocked teammate out of danger

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · team matches with DBNO · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Carry controls documented in Update 20.1

Use Limited Interact near a knocked player; the documented default is H.

Before you start: A living player, a knocked teammate and room to put them down.

1. Check your Limited Interact binding in Settings.
2. Identify nearby cover and check for enemies before approaching.
3. Stop sprinting beside the knocked teammate.
4. Use Limited Interact to pick them up.
5. Move to the chosen clear space and use the same binding to put them down.
6. Reassess the area before beginning the normal revive interaction.

Success check: The teammate is placed at the intended position.

Troubleshooting: Carry does not start? Stop sprinting and check the custom binding. Cannot put them down? Move to a space with sufficient clearance.

Choosing cover is tactical advice. Carrying does not guarantee a safe rescue; current on-screen prompts take priority.

## Use a ground item when your bag is full

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · supported modes · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Feature documented in Update 43.1

Hold the shown Limited Interact control on an eligible ground item.

Before you start: The ground-item-use feature and an item usable in your current state.

1. Find an eligible healing, boost or deployable item on the ground.
2. Check the interaction prompt before trying to collect it.
3. Hold H with default PC bindings, or your mapped Limited Interact control.
4. For deployables, check your position and facing direction first.

## Help a knocked teammate in water

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · team matches with DBNO swimming · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-20. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
DBNO swimming documented in PC Update 14.2

Stay at the surface or reach shore; underwater revives do not work.

Before you start: A knocked teammate, a living teammate and time before bleed-out.

1. As the knocked player, swim toward shore or a reachable surface position.
2. Tell your teammate where you are heading.
3. As the rescuer, check for danger before approaching.
4. Keep both players above water and begin the revive.
5. Avoid moving during the revive, then move to safer ground.

Success check: The revive completes without either player submerging.

Troubleshooting: Revive cancels? Submerging or moving too much interrupts it. Knocked player dying quickly? DBNO does not refill air; drowning and bleed-out still apply.

Stop beside a usable pump and hold the driver’s Interact control.

Before you start: A vehicle missing fuel, a working pump and the driver’s seat.

1. Find an undamaged pump and check the surrounding area.

## Measure progress in the Aim Lab

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · Training Mode · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Training Record feature · documented in Update 24.2

Complete an Aim Lab session, then use Training Record to inspect your score and accuracy.

Before you start: PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS on PC with Training Mode and the Aim/Sound Lab available.

1. Enter Training Mode and complete an Aim Lab session.
2. Open Training Record beside the Exit Training Mode control.
3. Read your score, accuracy and hit distribution.
4. Repeat with the same weapon and setup, changing only one practice variable at a time.
5. Compare recent sessions before deciding whether the change helped.

Success check: You can compare recorded practice results rather than relying only on how the session felt.

Troubleshooting: Look in Training Mode rather than the match-history screen. Different drills or weapons make direct comparisons less useful.

Records were documented in Update 24.2. A higher practice score does not guarantee better match results; the comparison routine is GameBreak advice.
