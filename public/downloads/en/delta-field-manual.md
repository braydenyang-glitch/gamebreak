# GameBreak — Delta Force

Operations is an extraction mode: your route, equipment budget and decision to leave matter together. This starting plan focuses on a small objective, affordable gear and learning one map before chasing expensive loot.

## Match the gun, ammunition and engagement

Build a repeatable starting setup instead of judging a weapon only by its price or rarity.

PC · Operations. Check regional client descriptions; Warfare rules are separate.

1. Open the weapon and ammunition details; verify caliber compatibility and the displayed penetration information.
2. Use Gunsmith to compare one attachment at a time; keep a simple configuration you can afford to replace.
3. Before a fight, identify cover and a retreat route; avoid testing an unfamiliar weapon in open ground.
4. After the raid, note whether ammunition, recoil control or positioning caused the problem; change just that part next time.

Operations and Warfare balance can differ. No damage breakpoint or best build is claimed here.

## Build a squad plan, not three solo routes

Use operator descriptions and clear markers to decide who checks ahead, who supports and when everybody leaves.

PC · Operations. Check regional client descriptions; Warfare rules are separate.

1. Read each chosen operator’s Operations ability text; do not copy a Warfare role description.
2. Agree who scouts the next approach and who watches the team’s rear while someone searches a container.
3. Customize the Operations marker wheel outside a match, then use short calls for threats, supplies and movement.
4. For a recon operator such as Morse, report what the ability actually detected; keep an escape option if information is incomplete.
5. Set a regroup point before splitting briefly; leave together when ammunition or medical supplies become a shared concern.

Ability ranges and cooldowns change with updates; read the current client rather than assuming permanent detection.

## Protect the raid budget and sort your loot

Separate storage, carried equipment and mission materials so one failed raid does not end your next attempt.

PC · Operations. Check regional client descriptions; Warfare rules are separate.

1. Keep spare gear in Stash; put only the equipment for this objective into your Loadout.
2. Check Safe Box eligibility and available space before placing valuable items there.
3. If you own a Gear Ticket, inspect its supplied kit and restrictions before spending extra Tekniq Alloy.
4. After extraction, reserve required materials, refill essentials and sell only items you have deliberately classified as spare.

Carried loot can be lost when extraction fails; Safe Box protection has item and access restrictions.

## Read the map before choosing the loot route

Use the official map tool as preparation, then confirm extraction conditions inside the actual raid.

PC · Operations. Check regional client descriptions; Warfare rules are separate.

1. Select the correct Operations map and difficulty in the official map tool; begin with one map you want to learn.
2. Find your objective and nearby map markers; sketch a short route instead of crossing the entire map for loot.
3. In the raid, inspect the extraction list, requirements and remaining time; identify a second option when available.
4. Recalculate the route after injuries, gunfire or a full backpack; a completed small objective is a reason to leave.

Map availability and extraction rules can change. An archived route is not a guarantee that an exit is open.

## Set up PC audio, gadget controls and markers

These are client settings, not console commands. Change one option, test its behavior, then keep or revert it.

PC · Operations. Check regional client descriptions; Warfare rules are separate.

1. With headphones, find Settings → Audio → HRTF and compare directional sound before committing to the option.
2. In Control & Combat, inspect Quick Throw, Hold and Default for supported tactical gadgets.
3. Try Flash Protection if bright flash effects are uncomfortable; it changes the flash presentation rather than removing the effect.
4. Set the marker wheel outside combat and rehearse a threat ping and a movement ping before your next raid.

These options were documented in official PC updates; names and availability may differ by regional client or input device.

## Connect Department missions to Black Site upgrades

Make an upgrade plan around an actual supply need instead of producing items only because a recipe is available.

PC · Operations. Check regional client descriptions; Warfare rules are separate.

1. Choose one Department mission and read its completion requirements before entering a raid.
2. Inspect the next Supply Station or Black Site requirement; keep a short list of materials you still need.
3. Compare a Black Site Workbench, Pharmacy or Armor Bench recipe with the supplies you actually use.
4. Check recipe cost and completion time in the client, then collect the result and update your material list.

Season missions, access items and recipes can change. This is a planning routine, not a guaranteed profit method.
