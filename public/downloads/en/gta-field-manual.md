# GameBreak — Grand Theft Auto V

GTA V Story Mode follows Michael, Franklin and Trevor through Los Santos and Blaine County. Learn driving, cover and mission objectives before chasing completion rewards. This portal covers the single-player campaign; GTA Online businesses, currencies and seasonal events belong to a different mode.

## Read the map and move the story forward

Use protagonist-specific mission markers to choose a clear next objective.

GTA V Story Mode • PC / PlayStation / Xbox. Not GTA Online; controls depend on platform. Sources reviewed, not playtested.

1. Finish the opening tutorials and read the current on-screen objective. A chase, shoot-out and meeting can require different actions even within one mission.
2. On the map, blue mission markers belong to Michael, green to Franklin and orange to Trevor. Select the protagonist who can start your chosen marker.
3. If your chosen mission is unavailable, check the other unlocked protagonists and pending setup tasks. Do not assume a missing icon is a broken save.
4. After a successful session, keep a manual save. Record the last completed mission so a walkthrough can start from the right point without revealing later plot.

Mission order can branch, and completion checklists include optional activities. A 100% checklist is not the same as the next story mission.

## Survive a shoot-out with cover and reload discipline

Plan a protected position before aiming; avoid crossing open ground while reloading.

GTA V Story Mode • PC / PlayStation / Xbox. Not GTA Online; controls depend on platform. Sources reviewed, not playtested.

1. Check the cover binding in Settings and practice entering and leaving cover beside a solid wall. Verify the camera still shows your escape route.
2. Use the weapon wheel to pick a suitable available weapon before exposing yourself. Avoid changing your entire loadout while enemies have a clear angle.
3. Watch both magazine ammunition and reserve ammunition. Reload behind cover before moving, rather than discovering an empty magazine in the next exchange.
4. Check health and armor between encounters. Ammu-Nation sells armor; replacing it before a difficult mission is more reliable than expecting cover to absorb every hit.

Armor is not protection from every damage type. Key bindings and aiming options vary by platform and user settings.

## Spend story cash on useful equipment first

Build a small preparation routine without relying on Online-only businesses or money exploits.

GTA V Story Mode • PC / PlayStation / Xbox. Not GTA Online; controls depend on platform. Sources reviewed, not playtested.

1. Check the active protagonist’s balance before shopping. Plan purchases for that character instead of assuming all three share one wallet.
2. Visit Ammu-Nation for available weapons, ammunition and armor. Buy ammunition for a weapon you actually use before paying for cosmetic upgrades.
3. Use a store with a shooting range when you want focused practice. Try one weapon class at a time and compare how confidently you can aim.
4. Keep a reserve before optional vehicle or property purchases. Recommended habit: save, inspect the price and purpose, then decide whether it helps your next goal.

This is a spending plan, not a guaranteed profit route. Story Mode stock tips and heist rewards require mission-specific research.

## Use a waypoint, then escape the search cones

Separate ordinary navigation from police evasion so the route does not lead you into another patrol.

GTA V Story Mode • PC / PlayStation / Xbox. Not GTA Online; controls depend on platform. Sources reviewed, not playtested.

1. Before driving, choose a destination on the map or through quick GPS. Check whether you need to reach a street entrance rather than the nearest straight-line point.
2. During a pursuit, first break the police line of sight. When the game enters search mode, use the radar cones to see areas to avoid.
3. Choose a quiet side route or covered hiding position and watch approaching patrols. A GPS line is a route to a destination, not a promise of safety.
4. Stay unseen until the wanted stars disappear. Being spotted starts active pursuit again; do not return to shopping or sightseeing while the search is still running.

Mission scripts can impose special wanted-level rules. This is ordinary free-roam evasion, not a guarantee that every mission permits escape immediately.

## Try Story Mode cheats on a separate save

Two simple PC examples, with clear save and achievement limits.

GTA V Story Mode • PC / PlayStation / Xbox. Not GTA Online; controls depend on platform. Sources reviewed, not playtested.

1. Create a manual save before testing. Use Story Mode free roam; these built-in codes are not GTA Online commands.
2. On PC, open the cheat input with the tilde key (~), type TURTLE and confirm. This restores health and armor when the cheat is available.
3. LAWYERUP lowers the wanted level by one star per use. Check the displayed result rather than assuming the entire pursuit has ended.
4. If the game reports that a cheat is unavailable, leave that activity before trying again. Reload your clean save or restart after the experiment.

Cheats temporarily disable achievements/trophies; Rockstar says reloading or rebooting resets cheats. Console button sequences differ, so PC words are not controller instructions.

## Use each protagonist’s specialty

Story Mode teamwork means switching playable characters when the mission allows it.

GTA V Story Mode • PC / PlayStation / Xbox. Not GTA Online; controls depend on platform. Sources reviewed, not playtested.

1. Read the switching prompt during shared missions. Change to the character handling the current problem rather than remaining with your favorite by habit.
2. Michael’s ability slows time in gunfights. Plan your aim before using it so the limited meter supports a difficult shot or crowded exchange.
3. Franklin’s ability slows time while driving road vehicles. Use it when approaching a difficult turn or traffic gap, then continue reading the road.
4. Trevor’s rage strengthens his combat effectiveness. Watch the special-ability meter and prepare cover before it empties rather than relying on it for the entire fight.

Characters unlock through the story, and switching can be restricted. These are single-player abilities, not multiplayer classes or a three-player co-op mode.
