Before you start: Story Mode and an earlier save to keep.

## Move a PC Story save to Enhanced

Grand Theft Auto V · Story Mode · PC Legacy → Enhanced · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
Rockstar PC migration workflow; no console transfer

Upload from Legacy; download in Enhanced only after checking the selected save.

Before you start: Both PC editions and the correct linked Rockstar account.

1. Confirm that the signed-in Rockstar account is linked to your intended PC platform account.
2. In Legacy, open Pause > Game > Upload Save Game.
3. Select the intended Story save and confirm its upload.
4. Wait for the successful-upload message.
5. Open Enhanced and choose Pause > Game > Download Save Game.
6. Check the selected save before confirming; a successful download uses this account’s Story migration.
7. Wait for confirmation and check the loaded progress.

Success check: Enhanced loads the intended Story progress.

Troubleshooting: Save is missing: Uploads remain available for 90 days; an expired upload may need uploading again. Expecting later Legacy progress to appear: Progress is not synced after migration. Using a console save: This workflow only supports PC Legacy to PC Enhanced.

One uploaded save per account. Once downloaded successfully, further Story migrations are unavailable on that account. Eligibility restrictions can apply.

Sources: [Rockstar Support · PC Story save migration](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/mmRgMVfuQC3xNzXK4Cq9b/migrating-your-story-mode-save-from-grand-theft-auto-v-legacy-to-grand-theft)

## Reduce intense first-person camera movement

Grand Theft Auto V · Story Mode · first-person-capable editions · PC / Console

Source checked: 2026-10-15. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
First-person settings; platform menus can differ

Reduce head movement and choose whether cover should use third person.

Before you start: An edition with First Person Mode.

1. Open Settings > Controls and locate the first-person options.
2. Turn First Person Head Bobbing off if running movement feels distracting.
3. Turn First Person Ragdoll off to reduce forced first-person motion when hit.
4. Turn First Person Combat Rolls off if rolling movement is uncomfortable.
5. Set First Person Third Person Cover on if you prefer a wider view while using cover.
6. Return to play and test walking, jogging and cover; adjust one setting at a time.

Success check: Camera movement and cover perspective match your preference.

Troubleshooting: Cover still stays first person: Recheck the specific cover option, rather than only the overall camera mode. Option missing: Confirm your edition supports first person; the original PS3/Xbox 360 versions do not use this feature set.

These are comfort preferences, not a guaranteed performance or aiming improvement. Use your edition’s displayed menu labels.

Sources: [Rockstar Game Tips · Playing with Perspective](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/25o2411812oa29/rockstar-game-tips-playing-with-perspective-in-gtav)

## Switch weapons with PC number shortcuts

Grand Theft Auto V · Story mode · PC keyboard · PC

Source checked: 2026-10-25. Unverified · Not playtested; source review does not prove latest-patch accuracy.
PC keyboard controls · publisher’s original PC guide

Rockstar’s PC guide lists 0–9 as default weapon-selection shortcuts. Repeating a category key cycles the weapons in that slot.

Before you start: GTAV on PC, keyboard controls and an owned weapon.

1. Open the key-binding settings and check your current weapon-selection keys.
2. Return to Story Mode when normal controls are available.
3. Press the relevant weapon-selection shortcut to choose the first weapon in that slot.
4. Press it again to cycle through other owned weapons in the same slot.

Success check: The intended weapon is equipped without repeatedly searching different categories.

Troubleshooting: Custom bindings override defaults. A category cannot select a weapon your character does not own.

The publisher guide dates from the original PC release. Follow your current bindings; this is not a controller layout or GTA Online loadout guide.
