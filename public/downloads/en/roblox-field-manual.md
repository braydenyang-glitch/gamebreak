# GameBreak — Roblox

This Roblox section focuses on LSPLASH’s DOORS, not every Roblox experience. In the Hotel, progress comes from searching rooms, reading entity warnings and solving obstacles. These guides explain standard survival responses and team habits. Updates, events and modifiers can change encounters; the official experience warns that mechanics may change.

## A clear route through the Hotel’s main obstacles

Understand the sequence without memorizing one fixed room layout or code.

Roblox DOORS • standard Hotel without modifiers. Community mechanics reviewed; not live playtesting or an event walkthrough.

1. Begin by finding the reception key and opening the first locked door. Search nearby containers and surfaces when later ordinary doors need a key.
2. At the Library, collect books that pair symbols with numbers. Read the paper’s symbol order to form that run’s five-digit padlock code.
3. Move carefully around Figure while solving the Library. Crouching reduces noise, but it does not make touching or approaching Figure safe.
4. At the Electrical Room finale, find the breaker switches and access the breaker room. Follow the panel’s displayed switch pattern to restore power and reach the exit.

The Library code is generated for the run. Special modes and later floors have different rules; do not reuse this as a Mines or event walkthrough.

## Rush, Hide and Seek need different responses

DOORS survival is about recognizing the threat, not applying one hiding trick to everything.

Roblox DOORS • standard Hotel without modifiers. Community mechanics reviewed; not live playtesting or an event walkthrough.

1. On entering a room, identify a usable hiding place. When Rush’s approach is signaled by its sound and, where present, flickering lights, prepare to hide.
2. Hide before Rush passes, then leave when that attack is over and the route is safe. Avoid waiting inside indefinitely because Hide can force you out.
3. During Seek’s Hotel chase, keep moving toward Guiding Light’s marked path. Crouch through the indicated low gaps rather than stopping to search drawers.
4. In the final chase corridor, steer around fire and reaching arms. Watch the next clear section of path instead of repeatedly turning to look behind.

Do not use a fixed seconds-to-hide rule: warnings, rooms and modifiers differ. An Ambush encounter can rebound and needs its own response.

## Manage light, lockpicks and two currencies

Save tools for a real obstacle and distinguish in-run Gold from Knobs.

Roblox DOORS • standard Hotel without modifiers. Community mechanics reviewed; not live playtesting or an event walkthrough.

1. Inspect drawers and surfaces for supplies while the room is safe. Keep enough time to respond to an approaching entity instead of finishing every container.
2. Switch a flashlight off when the area is readable without it. Batteries replenish compatible lights, so check the current charge before deciding who needs one.
3. A lockpick is consumed when used on a supported lock. Keep it for a difficult key search; it cannot solve the Library padlock or a Skeleton Key lock.
4. Gold found during the run can buy items at Jeff’s shop. Knobs are a separate progression currency used for pre-run purchases; check the shop’s currency before buying.

Stock, prices and item behavior can change. This guide deliberately gives no fixed price, drop rate or guaranteed shop inventory.

## Search rooms without losing the route

Track door numbers and make a quick search plan before checking every corner.

Roblox DOORS • standard Hotel without modifiers. Community mechanics reviewed; not live playtesting or an event walkthrough.

1. Read and remember the number of the door you enter. Use that sequence to identify the expected next number when multiple numbered exits appear.
2. For a locked ordinary door, check drawers and nearby surfaces systematically. Search one side of the room first so you know which areas remain unchecked.
3. Watch for Guiding Light hints when a required object is difficult to find. Treat hints as help with the current obstacle, not protection from enemies.
4. If Dupe has scrambled labels or you lose count, stop before touching a suspicious exit and compare the group’s remembered number. Do not test every door blindly.

Modifiers may weaken or remove hints. Room layouts vary, so a screenshot route from one run is not a fixed route for every run.

## Controls and settings: learn them before the chase

Use the game’s real interaction prompts; DOORS is not a Minecraft-style command sandbox.

Roblox DOORS • standard Hotel without modifiers. Community mechanics reviewed; not live playtesting or an event walkthrough.

1. On PC, use WASD to move and E for ordinary interactions. Practice collecting an object and holding the interaction when an unlocking prompt requires it.
2. Practice crouching with C on the default PC layout, then standing again. Low chase obstacles are a poor place to discover an unfamiliar button.
3. On mobile, use the visible interact and crouch buttons and tap an inventory slot to select an item. Follow controller glyphs on console.
4. Open the in-game settings and adjust camera sensitivity and audio to a readable, responsive setup. Rehearse item switching before entering an unfamiliar section.

Bindings and interfaces can change. No public survival command such as /skip-entity is provided here; follow the actual on-screen prompts.

## Move as a team without sharing one hiding spot

Use short callouts and a regrouping habit to reduce avoidable team mistakes.

Roblox DOORS • standard Hotel without modifiers. Community mechanics reviewed; not live playtesting or an event walkthrough.

1. Suggested roles: one player calls the next door number while others check opposite sides for supplies. Confirm everyone is ready before opening several rooms ahead.
2. Identify separate hiding positions before danger arrives. A shouted warning is more useful when each teammate already knows which unoccupied spot to use.
3. In the Library, share discovered symbol-number pairs and the paper order. Let one player enter the assembled code while others remain clear of Figure.
4. Regroup after a chase or long search. Falling several rooms behind can cause Void to return a player to the group with damage, so monitor slower teammates.

These roles are suggested coordination habits, not official character classes. Do not promise that a teammate can always revive you or that a hiding spot is permanently safe.
