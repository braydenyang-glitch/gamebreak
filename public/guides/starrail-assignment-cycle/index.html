Collect and restart Assignments

Make the passive material routine easy to check.

Honkai: Star Rail · Assignments

Unverified · Claim All workflow documented in Version 2.1

Quick answer

Review your stock, claim finished returns and redispatch deliberately.

Before you start

Assignments available on your account.

Steps

  1. Open Assignments.
  2. Inspect the material stock shown for the assignments you use.
  3. Check which returns are ready.
  4. Choose Claim All to collect completed returns.
  5. Use the redispatch option, then confirm which assignments are running.

Collected materials are reflected in stock and the intended assignments are running.

Nothing to claim: Check completion state instead of assuming materials were lost. Wrong materials keep arriving: Review the selected assignment before redispatching.

Menu wording may evolve. No fixed assignment duration, capacity or reward quantity is assumed.
