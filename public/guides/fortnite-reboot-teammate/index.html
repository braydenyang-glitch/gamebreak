Use a Reboot Card to return a teammate

Distinguish an eliminated teammate from a teammate who can still be revived.

Fortnite · Battle Royale · supported team playlists

Unverified · Battle Royale reboot fundamentals · introduced in v8.30; current timers untested

Quick answer

When your playlist provides Reboot Cards, collect your eliminated teammate’s card and use an available Reboot Van.

Before you start

A team playlist with the reboot mechanic and a collectible teammate card.

Steps

  1. Check the eliminated teammate’s card marker and current expiry timer.
  2. Collect the card before it expires if you can reach it safely.
  3. Locate a usable Reboot Van and check its current availability.
  4. Follow the van’s interaction prompt until the reboot completes.

The eliminated teammate returns to play through the reboot.

An unavailable van may be cooling down; some playlists do not use this mechanic.

The source establishes the original mechanic. Do not use its old timer, starting gear or map locations as current values. Reload has different reboot rules.
