Check stored Waveplate Crystals before farming

See whether stored energy can cover the reward you want.

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · standard overworld and menus

Unverified · Waveplate Crystal storage documented in Version 1.2

Quick answer

Read both energy counts on the Map, then exchange stored Crystals if needed.

Before you start

Waveplate Crystals already stored and an available reward activity.

Steps

  1. Open the Map and inspect the Waveplate and Waveplate Crystal counts.
  2. Choose a challenge whose materials you actually need.
  3. Compare its reward cost with your available Waveplates.
  4. If short, use the energy replenishment prompts to exchange stored Crystals.
  5. Check the new total before entering or claiming the reward.

You know whether your stored energy covers the planned claim.

No Crystals stored? They accumulate after normal Waveplates reach their cap. Not enough energy? Exchange only the stored amount you have; this is not unlimited energy.

Counts and the exchange mechanic were checked in archived publisher text. The live confirmation prompts determine available amounts.
