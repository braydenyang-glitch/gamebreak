Start a two-player split-screen match

Set up a supported console session with two separate player accounts.

Fortnite · Battle Royale / Zero Build · supported PlayStation and Xbox consoles

Unverified · Publisher local-multiplayer compatibility help

Quick answer

Connect the second controller in the lobby and follow the second-player sign-in prompts.

Before you start

Two controllers, separate player accounts, the same language and stable internet.

Steps

  1. Launch Fortnite and enter the lobby.
  2. Connect the second controller.
  3. Choose the second player’s console account; guest or Quick Play accounts do not work.
  4. Follow the on-screen Epic account linking or sign-in prompts.
  5. Check that both players appear in the lobby.
  6. Choose a supported local-multiplayer Duos or Squads playlist and start.

The match displays both players on split screen.

Second screen missing in menus? Split screen appears during the match, not every submenu. Unsupported playlist or device? This feature excludes PC, mobile, Switch, Solos and Ranked.

If either player leaves or disconnects, the split-screen session ends.
