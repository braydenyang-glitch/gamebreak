Relocate with an Emergency Pickup

Choose clear ground and a landing plan before deploying the balloon.

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · PC · eligible Battle Royale maps and phases

Unverified · Emergency Pickup documented in Update 11.1

Quick answer

Deploy in an open area, attach to the rope, then detach above your chosen landing route.

Before you start

An Emergency Pickup and an eligible location in the current match.

Steps

  1. Check the zone and choose a destination before deploying.
  2. Find open ground with clear sky and no nearby obstacles.
  3. Deploy the item and attach yourself to the rope.
  4. Keep watching the surroundings while waiting for the plane.
  5. During the flight, choose a safe place to detach.
  6. Detach with enough height to parachute and steer toward cover.

You land at the planned location rather than remaining on the rope.

Deployment fails? Check sky clearance, terrain and phase restrictions. Taking fire in transit? The rope does not protect you, and you cannot shoot while attached.

Availability depends on the current map, playlist and phase. This guide does not reuse the 2021 map-rotation list.
