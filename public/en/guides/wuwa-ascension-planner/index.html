Plan one Resonator’s next upgrades

Use the built-in plan to locate missing materials across the character’s equipment.

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · standard overworld and menus

Unverified · Resonator upgrade planner documented in Version 2.7

Quick answer

Open Terminal → Resonators → the ascension planner and review its modules.

Before you start

Access to the Resonator planner and a character you want to improve.

Steps

  1. Open Terminal → Resonators and enter the ascension planner.
  2. Select the Resonator you want to develop.
  3. Review the Resonator, weapon, Echo and skill modules.
  4. Choose one achievable next upgrade instead of farming everything at once.
  5. Use the material-source shortcut for the missing requirement.
  6. Return after gathering it and check your progress.

You have a clear next upgrade and know its material source.

Recommendations differ from another account? The plan considers your highest SOL3 Phase. Preview character details missing? Some preview plans become available closer to release.

The official German article was readable; the English counterpart returned no extractable text. Menu translations may differ.
