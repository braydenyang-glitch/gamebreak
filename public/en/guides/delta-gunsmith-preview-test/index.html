Quick answer

Keep a baseline, try one change, then decide whether to apply the trial build.

Before you start

Access to Gunsmith and the Firing Range.

Steps

  1. Open the weapon in Gunsmith and note its starting configuration.
  2. Change one compatible attachment so the comparison stays clear.
  3. Use Preview Mode’s Calibrate option if relevant.
  4. Enter the Firing Range and test the handling at a consistent distance.
  5. Return to Gunsmith; the documented workflow preserves the trial configuration.
  6. Choose whether to apply the trial loadout, then check the final equipment before deploying.

You have chosen a configuration after testing its handling.

Build looks different after leaving: Confirm whether you applied the trial loadout. Attachment will not fit: Check the weapon’s compatibility and requirements instead of assuming every attachment fits.

This is a comparison routine, not a meta ranking. Balance values and regional features may change; inspect your current client.
