Use a Gear Ticket for a prepared kit

Review a supplied loadout before spending on a custom one.

Delta Force · Operations · Garena reference

Unverified · Garena documentation · check your client

Quick answer

A Gear Ticket supplies a kit including protection, weapons, ammunition and medical items.

Before you start

An available eligible Gear Ticket.

Steps

  1. Check whether a Gear Ticket is available in your client.
  2. Inspect its supplied kit and any displayed restrictions.
  3. Confirm the ammunition and medical items before entering Operations.

You understand the kit before entering.

Check ticket requirements if it cannot be selected.

Garena documents tickets from missions and events. Availability is not guaranteed for every account or service.
