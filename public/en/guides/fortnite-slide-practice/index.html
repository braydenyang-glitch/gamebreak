Slide through a short exposed route

Learn the input on safe ground before using it during a fight.

Fortnite · Battle Royale / Zero Build · sliding

Unverified · Sliding documented December 2021

Quick answer

Hold Crouch while running to start a slide.

Before you start

A clear route and a working Crouch binding.

Steps

  1. Find a short, unobstructed route in a quiet area.
  2. Identify cover or a safe stopping point at its end.
  3. Start running, then hold your Crouch control.
  4. Keep the camera facing the route while sliding.
  5. Try aiming during a second attempt after the movement feels familiar.
  6. Compare the slide with a normal run before choosing it in combat.

You can trigger a slide deliberately and keep track of your destination.

Only crouching? Begin running before holding the control. On flat ground? Sliding does not require a downhill slope.

Source checked; not gameplay tested. Use the current in-game prompts if controls differ.
