Prepare food for an overworld farming route

Choose the effect for the resource you need instead of eating at random.

Wuthering Waves · Wuthering Waves · standard overworld and menus

Unverified · Farming dishes documented in Version 1.1

Quick answer

Compare Echo-drop food with enemy-material food before starting the route.

Before you start

The relevant learned recipe, ingredients and access to cooking.

Steps

  1. Decide whether you need Echo drops or ordinary enemy materials.
  2. Inspect the recipe and effect before spending ingredients.
  3. For Echo farming, check Sweet & Sour Pork or Lemon-Braised Pork.
  4. For enemy materials, check Caltrop Stir Fry or Stuffed Tofu.
  5. Prepare and use the selected dish before the relevant overworld fights.
  6. Read its current duration and limits in the item description.

The active food effect matches the type of farming you planned.

Recipe missing? The update points players to Mt. Firmament for these recipes. No guaranteed Echo? An increased drop chance does not guarantee every kill drops one.

Source checked; not gameplay tested. Use the current in-game prompts if controls differ.
