Prepare three teams for Anomaly Arbitration

Avoid resetting Knight progress by reusing locked equipment.

Honkai: Star Rail · Equilibrium 6 · Anomaly Arbitration entry

Unverified · Equilibrium 6 · Anomaly Arbitration entry

Quick answer

Cleared Knight teams cannot share characters, Light Cones or relics.

Before you start

Equilibrium 6 and the mode’s highest-stage star requirements.

Steps

  1. Read the entry requirements and confirm your qualifying clears.
  2. Prepare three separate Knight teams, including separate equipment.
  3. Read each Knight’s traits before assigning a team.
  4. Clear the three Knight challenges with those planned teams.
  5. Choose the available Arbitral Quadrant benefit for the King.
  6. Attempt the King and review the result.

Three Knight clears remain recorded while you prepare for the King.

Check the two troubleshooting cases below.

Entry requires maximum stars on the highest Memory of Chaos, Pure Fiction and Apocalyptic Shadow stages; qualifying versions may differ.
