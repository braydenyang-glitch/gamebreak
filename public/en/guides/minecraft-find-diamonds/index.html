Prepare a diamond-mining trip

Use the right pickaxe and coordinates before digging deeper.

Minecraft · Java / Bedrock · post-Caves & Cliffs terrain

Unverified · Java / Bedrock · post-1.18 terrain principles; current patch not tested

Quick answer

Bring an iron pickaxe and use coordinates to explore deep Overworld terrain. Do not follow old Y=11 advice as a universal rule.

Before you start

An iron pickaxe, food, torches, blocks and a route back to your base.

Steps

  1. Record your base coordinates before leaving.
  2. In Java, use F3 and read XYZ. In Bedrock, enable Show Coordinates under the world’s Game settings.
  3. Bring an iron pickaxe: a stone pickaxe is not the upgrade step used to collect diamonds.
  4. Descend with a staircase or another route you can retrace. Watch for open drops and lava.
  5. Search deep terrain, including buried blocks. The post-1.18 distribution puts more diamond ore lower down and reduces exposed-air ore.
  6. Mine discovered diamond ore with your iron pickaxe and return before your supplies run out.

You find diamond ore, collect its drop with a suitable pickaxe and can retrace your route.

Check your world generation/version and search beyond visible cave walls. No route guarantees a diamond deposit.

This gives preparation and distribution principles, not a measured best Y-level or diamonds-per-hour claim. Modpacks and older terrain can differ.
